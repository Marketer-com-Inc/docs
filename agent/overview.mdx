The Marketer AI agent is your always-on media buyer — reading every ad account across Meta, Google, Snapchat, and ChatGPT Ads continuously, making optimizations in real time, and surfacing decisions for you only when a human judgment call is required. Most agencies review performance once a week. Marketer reviews it every hour of every day, catching budget bleed at 2 a.m., reallocating spend before your morning coffee, and building a living record of what's working and what isn't — so you walk into every week with momentum, not damage control.

## How It Works

The agent connects directly to your ad accounts and operates in a continuous read-act-report loop. It doesn't wait for you to log in. When it detects a signal worth acting on — a ROAS drop, a creative going stale, a keyword draining spend — it acts, then tells you what it did and why.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read">
    The agent ingests performance data from every connected ad account — spend, ROAS, CTR, impression share, keyword quality scores, and more — on an ongoing basis, 24 hours a day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide">
    It evaluates signals against your goals and account history. When performance crosses a threshold, it generates a recommended action: pause, shift budget, launch, clean, or consolidate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Act or Surface">
    For routine optimizations within pre-approved parameters, the agent acts immediately. For material changes — significant budget shifts, new campaign launches, pausing a top-line ad set — it queues the action for your approval before executing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Report">
    Every action is logged in the Campaign Activity panel and summarized in your daily Slack morning brief. You always know exactly what changed, when, and why.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## A Real Example

Here's a signal the agent caught and acted on for a protein bar brand:

> **"ROAS on Cold Prospecting dropped 23%. Agent suggests pausing 2 adsets and pushing energy angle."**

The gut-health creative angle had been running for three weeks. ROAS had been declining. The agent detected the trend, cross-referenced it against the brand's top-performing angles, and concluded the energy angle was the better allocation. It queued the action, notified the team in Slack, and waited for approval — all before anyone opened their laptop.

## 168 Optimizations a Week

A typical agency engagement means one weekly call, one weekly report, and a handful of manual changes made by someone context-switching between dozens of accounts. Marketer makes **168 optimizations a week** — every hour of every day — because the agent never clocks out, never gets distracted, and never misses a metric shift at 11 p.m. on a Sunday.

That's not a feature comparison. It's a structural difference in how your ad accounts get managed.

<Note>
  The agent acts autonomously on routine optimizations, but every material action — significant budget shifts, new launches, pausing key campaigns — requires your approval before going live. You stay in control of the decisions that matter.
</Note>

## Research Engine

The agent doesn't just react to your account data. It reads the market. Connected to Reddit signals, Google Trends, and a continuously updated competitor insights feed, Marketer surfaces what's resonating in your category right now — so when it suggests pushing a new angle, that recommendation is grounded in real audience behavior, not a hunch.

<Tip>
  The research engine updates continuously. If a competitor launches a new creative hook or a trend spikes on Reddit in your category, the agent incorporates that signal into its recommendations — often before you've had a chance to notice it yourself.
</Tip>
