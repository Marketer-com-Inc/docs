Connecting Marketer to Slack turns your ad account management into something you can do from your phone, between meetings, without ever opening Ads Manager. Instead of logging into a dashboard to find out what changed overnight, you wake up to a morning brief in Slack that tells you exactly what the agent did, what's working, and what needs your attention. When the agent needs a decision from you, it pings you directly — with context, a recommendation, and approve/reject buttons right in the message. You stay in control of every material action without being tethered to a desktop.

## Connect Slack to Marketer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Integrations">
    In your Marketer dashboard, navigate to **Settings** and select the **Integrations** tab. You'll see all available integrations, with Slack listed at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect Slack">
    Select **Connect Slack** to begin the authorization flow. Marketer will redirect you to Slack's OAuth screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize Marketer in your Slack workspace">
    Review the permissions Marketer requests — it needs permission to post messages and read channel names — and click **Allow**. You'll be redirected back to your Marketer dashboard once authorization is complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your channels">
    Select the Slack channel where you want to receive your **morning brief** and the channel (which can be the same or different) for **agent action alerts and performance notifications**. You can update these at any time in Settings → Integrations.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You Receive in Slack

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Morning Brief">
    Every morning, Marketer sends a daily summary to your chosen Slack channel. The brief covers:

    - **Overnight activity** — every action the agent took while you were offline, with timestamps and rationale
    - **What changed** — significant shifts in ROAS, CTR, spend pacing, or creative performance since your last brief
    - **What needs your attention** — a prioritized list of actions queued for your approval and any metrics that warrant a closer look

    The morning brief arrives before your workday starts — so by the time you're at your desk, you already know the state of your accounts.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Action Alerts">
    When the agent identifies a material action that requires your approval — pausing a key ad set, launching a new campaign, shifting significant budget — it sends an action alert directly to Slack. The message includes:

    - What triggered the action (the specific metric signal)
    - What the agent recommends doing and why
    - The expected impact of approving the action
    - Inline **[Approve]**, **[Edit]**, and **[No]** buttons so you can respond without leaving Slack

    You don't need to open the dashboard to action these. Respond directly in Slack and the agent executes (or stands down) immediately.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Performance Alerts">
    If a metric drops below your configured threshold — ROAS on a key campaign, CTR on your top creative, spend pacing off-track on a time-sensitive campaign — Marketer sends a performance alert to Slack.

    Performance alerts are distinct from action alerts: they're informational, telling you what's happening, rather than asking for a decision. If the agent has already queued a response action, that action alert will accompany or follow the performance alert.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Example Slack Interaction

Here's what an agent action alert looks like in your Slack channel:

<Note>
**📊 Marketer — Action Required**

**ROAS on Cold Prospecting dropped 23%.**
I've identified 2 underperforming adsets in the gut-health angle driving the decline. Recommend pausing both and shifting $400 to the energy angle, which is currently running at 3.8× ROAS.

**Proposed actions:**
- Pause: *Cold Prospecting — Gut Health v2* and *Cold Prospecting — Gut Health v4*
- Shift $400 from gut-health budget to *Cold Prospecting — Energy Angle*

**[ ✅ Approve ] [ ✏️ Edit ] [ ❌ No ]**
</Note>

Tap **Approve** and both actions execute against the live account within seconds. Tap **Edit** to modify the budget amount or the specific ad sets before approving. Tap **No** to reject the recommendation — the agent logs your decision and continues monitoring.

## Managing Campaigns From Mobile

<Tip>
  The Slack integration is built for mobile. Every morning brief and action alert is formatted to be readable and actionable on a phone screen — so you can run your ad accounts from anywhere. Approve a budget shift from the school run. Review the overnight activity log on the train. You don't need to be at a desk to stay on top of your accounts.
</Tip>

## Alert Frequency

<Note>
  Marketer is designed to reduce noise, not add to it. The agent handles hundreds of micro-optimizations each week autonomously — you don't get a Slack ping for every negative keyword removed or every minor bid adjustment. You receive alerts when a human decision is actually needed: material budget shifts, new campaign launches, significant performance drops, and actions outside the agent's pre-approved parameters. On a typical day, most brands receive one morning brief and zero to two action alerts.
</Note>
