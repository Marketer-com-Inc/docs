TRIP is the UK's fastest-growing soft drinks brand — a premium CBD and adaptogen beverage label founded in 2019 by Olivia Ferdi and Daniel Khoury. The brand's origin story is personal: weeks before their wedding, Daniel was injured playing football and told he wouldn't walk down the aisle. He tried CBD and recovered in seven weeks. The couple left careers in law and finance and launched TRIP around the idea of "a little bit of calm in everyday chaos." Today, TRIP holds 88% of the UK CBD drinks market, is the sixth-largest carbonated drinks company in the UK, and is the top-selling drink on TikTok. It is stocked in 11,000+ locations worldwide, including Sainsbury's, Boots, Holland & Barrett, Ocado, Selfridges, Harrods, and Soho House globally.

## The Challenge

TRIP's product range expanded well beyond CBD drinks into magnesium drinks, Mindful Blend (with Lion's Mane and Ashwagandha), powders, and gummies — each with its own positioning, each across multiple channels and markets. UK retail. US launch. Carrefour in France. Soho House globally. DTC, hospitality, paid social, and out-of-home.

The aesthetic-calming visual world TRIP is known for needed to ship at the speed of a viral soft drinks brand, across every geography and in every format, without the bottleneck of an agency-and-shoot cycle. For a brand where the look is the product, generic ad templates weren't an option — but neither was a production model that couldn't keep up.

## How Marketer Helps

Marketer became TRIP's creative engine through Ember. The full pastel visual world of the brand is now produced and iterated inside one AI-native creative production environment. Work runs across statics and video, across UK and US product lines, briefed in by a rotating bench of strategists, brand leads, and channel owners on TRIP's side.

**Ember** holds the brand consistently across everything it produces. The first major launch through the system was the electrolyte sachet line — a category Ember picked up end-to-end, including lifestyle imagery TRIP would not otherwise have commissioned a shoot for. From there the engine moved into summer heatwave creative, Amazon product video across the full lineup, email assets, and Meta creative built specifically to lift average order value on multipacks.

The freelancer bench TRIP previously leaned on for lifestyle work is being replaced by one system that carries the brand. Lower-tier launches that previously would have gone to market without a dedicated shoot now receive full creative coverage.

## Key Takeaways

<Tip>
  For premium lifestyle brands, creative volume and brand consistency are not in tension — they become mutually reinforcing when the production system is built around the brand's visual language from the start. TRIP's experience shows that the constraint isn't how much you can produce; it's whether the system producing at volume genuinely understands what the brand looks like.
</Tip>
