Marketer's ability to optimize performance depends on having a complete, real-time view of your business — ad spend, creative performance, store revenue, and customer lifecycle all in one place. Connecting your channels creates that single source of truth: the agent can see where money is being made, where it's being wasted, and what to do about it without you having to manually pull reports or cross-reference platforms. This page walks you through connecting each supported channel.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Meta">
    ## Connect Meta

    Connecting your Meta Business account gives Marketer access to your Facebook and Instagram ad accounts, including all existing campaigns, creatives, and audience data.

    **How to connect**

    1. In your Marketer dashboard, navigate to **Channels → Meta**.
    2. Click **Connect Meta** and log in with the Meta account that has admin access to your Business Manager.
    3. Select the ad accounts you want Marketer to manage and confirm access.

    **What Marketer reads and manages**

    - Active and paused campaigns, ad sets, and individual ads
    - Creative assets (images, videos, copy, headlines)
    - Custom audiences, lookalike audiences, and saved audiences
    - Pixel data and conversion events
    - Historical performance data (impressions, clicks, spend, conversions, ROAS)

    The agent uses this data to pause underperforming ad sets, shift budget between campaigns, adjust audience targeting, and launch new campaigns — all tied back to real revenue from your connected store.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Google">
    ## Connect Google Ads

    Connecting Google Ads gives Marketer visibility into your search, shopping, display, and performance max campaigns so the agent can optimize bids, budgets, and keywords continuously.

    **How to connect**

    1. In your Marketer dashboard, navigate to **Channels → Google**.
    2. Click **Connect Google Ads** and sign in with the Google account that manages your ad account.
    3. Grant the required permissions and select the Google Ads account(s) you want Marketer to manage.

    **What Marketer reads and manages**

    - All campaign types (Search, Shopping, Performance Max, Display)
    - Ad groups, keywords, and negative keyword lists
    - Search term reports and query-level performance data
    - Bid strategies and budget allocations
    - Historical performance data (clicks, impressions, conversions, cost-per-conversion)

    The agent cleans wasted keyword spend, adjusts bids based on conversion data, and restructures campaigns when performance data warrants it — making decisions based on your store's actual revenue, not just in-platform metrics.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Snapchat">
    ## Connect Snapchat

    Connecting your Snap Business account lets Marketer extend the same 24/7 optimization loop to your Snapchat campaigns.

    **How to connect**

    1. In your Marketer dashboard, navigate to **Channels → Snapchat**.
    2. Click **Connect Snapchat** and authenticate with your Snap Business Manager account.
    3. Select the ad accounts you want Marketer to manage and confirm access.

    **What Marketer reads and manages**

    - Active campaigns, ad sets, and individual ads
    - Creative assets and formats (Single Image, Video, Story, Collection)
    - Audience segments and targeting parameters
    - Snap Pixel events and conversion data
    - Historical performance data (impressions, swipe-ups, spend, conversions)

    Marketer applies the same optimization logic across Snapchat as it does on Meta and Google — pausing poor performers, reallocating budget, and feeding performance data back into Ember creative briefs.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Shopify">
    ## Connect Shopify

    Your Shopify store is the revenue ground truth that makes Marketer's campaign decisions meaningful. Connecting it means every budget move the agent makes is tied to actual orders and customer value — not just clicks.

    **How to connect**

    1. In your Marketer dashboard, navigate to **Channels → Store**.
    2. Select **Shopify** and click **Install on Shopify**.
    3. You'll be redirected to the Shopify App Store. Install the Marketer app and grant the requested permissions.

    **What Marketer syncs**

    - Product catalog (titles, descriptions, prices, variants, images)
    - Order history and revenue data
    - Customer records and purchase frequency
    - Collections and inventory status

    This data informs which products to promote in campaigns, which audiences are most valuable, and how to attribute ad spend to real revenue. Marketer also uses product catalog data to build Shopping campaigns and dynamic creatives automatically.

    <Info>
      WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce, and custom storefronts are also supported. Select your platform from the **Store** tab in the Channels section of your dashboard to see the specific connection flow.
    </Info>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Klaviyo">
    ## Connect Klaviyo

    Connecting Klaviyo brings your email and SMS lifecycle data into the same source of truth as your paid channels, so Marketer can coordinate messaging across the full customer journey — not just top-of-funnel ads.

    **How to connect**

    1. In your Marketer dashboard, navigate to **Channels → Klaviyo**.
    2. Click **Connect Klaviyo** and enter your Klaviyo API key. You can find this in your Klaviyo account under **Settings → API Keys**.
    3. Confirm access and select the lists and segments you want Marketer to work with.

    **What Marketer reads and uses**

    - Subscriber lists and segments
    - Flow performance data (open rates, click rates, revenue per recipient)
    - Campaign history and engagement data
    - Customer lifecycle stage (new, active, at-risk, lapsed)

    Marketer uses Klaviyo data to align paid re-targeting suppression with email flows, identify high-value segments to target or exclude in ad campaigns, and ensure lifecycle messaging and paid ads aren't working against each other.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  **Your data stays yours.** Marketer reads your ad account and store data to power the agent's decisions, but it never transfers your data to third parties or uses it for any purpose outside your account. You can disconnect any channel at any time from the **Channels** section of your dashboard.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Connecting Slack is handled separately from your ad channels and store. Once your channels are connected, head to [Slack Integration](/agent/slack-integration) to set up morning briefs and action approvals in Slack.
</Tip>

## What happens after you connect

Once a channel is connected, Marketer begins indexing your data immediately. Here's what that process looks like:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Data indexing">
    Marketer fetches your existing campaigns, creatives, analytics, audiences, and product catalog. For most accounts this takes a few minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Performance baseline">
    The agent builds a baseline understanding of what's working and what isn't — which campaigns are profitable, which ad sets are draining budget, and which audiences are converting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="First brief prepared">
    Marketer surfaces the first campaign brief in your dashboard (and in Slack, if connected), showing the actions it recommends and asking for your approval before making any changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continuous optimization begins">
    Once you approve the first brief, the agent starts running — making optimizations around the clock and delivering a morning brief each day with a summary of overnight activity and upcoming recommendations.
  </Step>
</Steps>
