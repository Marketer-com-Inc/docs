Platforms reward variety. Meta's algorithm favors fresh creative rotation; Google's Performance Max distributes across surfaces that each respond to different formats; Snapchat audiences convert differently on video than on static. Running a single format across all placements is one of the most common and costly creative mistakes in paid media. Ember covers all six major format categories inside one subscription, so you can match the right creative type to the right placement — without managing six separate vendors or production timelines. Every format goes through the same intelligence engine and the same senior designer review before it reaches your dashboard.

## Ad Creatives

Static display ads are the foundation of most performance programs. Ember produces hero formats, direct-response units, and promotional layouts designed to stop the scroll and drive action. These are built for conversion — clear hierarchy, sharp messaging, and CTAs that match the intent of your audience segment.

Ember's intelligence engine draws on patterns from over 1 million real ads to inform layout decisions, copy placement, and visual hierarchy. Senior designers then curate every output to make sure the execution matches your brand standards, not just a generic template.

**Best for:** Meta Feed, Instagram Feed, Google Display, retargeting campaigns, promotional pushes.

## Video Ads

Video consistently outperforms static for incremental ROAS — and Ember builds every video asset around a proven structural frame: **hook → body → CTA**. The hook is optimized for the first two seconds (the window that determines whether someone keeps watching). The body carries the value proposition. The CTA drives the action.

<Tip>
  In the SoftMoc case study, video creatives delivered a **5.43× incremental ROAS** compared to **4.31×** for static — a meaningful gap at scale. If you're running video and static in parallel, let performance data tell you where to weight your budget. Ember's performance loop surfaces that signal automatically.
</Tip>

Motion creative unlocks placements that static simply can't access — Reels, TikTok-style surfaces, YouTube pre-roll, and connected TV inventory. Ember produces video ads that are spec-compliant for each platform out of the box, so you're not re-exporting for every destination.

**Best for:** Meta Reels, Instagram Stories, YouTube, Snapchat Video, connected TV pre-roll.

## Product Shots

Product photography is one of the most expensive and time-consuming parts of a creative program to maintain — especially when you're managing a catalog with dozens or hundreds of SKUs. Ember generates isolated, studio-quality product shots at SKU level using a 5-step image generation pipeline: subject definition, style application, world and background construction, lighting calibration, and final detail refinement.

The result looks like it came off a professional product shoot — because the underlying model was trained on exactly that kind of output, and a senior designer signs off before delivery.

**Best for:** Product detail pages, catalog ads, shopping campaigns, email headers, organic product posts.

## AI UGC

User-generated content converts because it feels real — unpolished, peer-sourced, and authentic. But sourcing real UGC is slow, legally complex, and inconsistent. Ember's AI UGC format produces creator-realistic content that carries the visual and tonal cues of genuine UGC without the coordination overhead.

<Info>
  AI UGC is not about faking authenticity — it's about capturing the **aesthetic and register** of creator content (direct address, natural environments, conversational tone) in a format that's production-consistent and on-brand. Every output is reviewed by senior designers to make sure it reads as genuine, not generated.
</Info>

The format is particularly effective for beauty, wellness, and fashion categories where social proof and relatability drive conversion.

**Best for:** Meta Feed and Stories, TikTok-style placements, top-of-funnel awareness, DTC acquisition campaigns.

## Web Creative

Your paid media creative shouldn't stop at the ad unit — the landing page, banner, and display assets your audience sees after the click need to match the creative they responded to. Ember produces web creative assets including banners, landing page hero images, and display units sized for the placements you're actually running.

All web creative is spec-compliant for standard IAB display sizes and can be adapted for custom landing page layouts. Format and spec compliance is handled automatically — you don't need to manage export settings or resize assets manually.

**Best for:** Google Display Network, programmatic display, landing page hero images, on-site banners.

## Email & SMS

Email and SMS are high-intent channels — audiences who've opted in are already warm. Ember produces email header images and promotional banners designed for Klaviyo campaigns, built to match your brand visual system and the specific promotional angle of each send.

These assets aren't generic stock-photo headers. They're briefed specifically for your campaign — sale event, new arrival, seasonal push — and go through the same brand-compliance check as every other Ember output.

**Best for:** Klaviyo email campaigns, promotional headers, SMS visual assets, seasonal campaign sends.
