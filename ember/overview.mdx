Ember is Marketer's AI-powered ad creative product — built for brands that need creative volume without sacrificing quality. Where most AI tools hand you raw output and wish you luck, Ember pairs its intelligence engine with senior designers who review and curate every asset before it reaches you. The result is studio-quality creative at a pace and scale no agency or freelancer can match. Whether you're running performance campaigns on Meta, expanding into video, or covering six product categories at once, Ember handles the volume so your team can focus on strategy.

Ember covers every major creative surface inside a single subscription. You don't need separate vendors for static ads, video, and product photography — it's all handled in one place, briefed through the same workflow, and delivered to the same dashboard.

## What Makes Ember Different

Ember's intelligence engine is trained on over **1 million ads** of real performance data, which means it isn't guessing at what works — it's drawing on patterns across formats, industries, and platforms. That foundation is what separates Ember from a generic image generator.

But data alone doesn't produce great creative. Every output Ember generates is reviewed and curated by senior designers before it reaches your dashboard. The AI handles scale; the designers handle taste. You get both.

## You Own Everything

Every creative Ember produces belongs to you outright. There are no licensing restrictions, usage fees, or platform lock-in. You can run the assets on any channel, repurpose them for organic, hand them to your media buying team, or archive them for future campaigns — they're yours.

<Note>
  **Full ownership, no strings attached.** Unlike some AI creative platforms, Ember imposes no usage windows, seat-based licensing fees, or channel restrictions on outputs. Once delivered, creatives are yours permanently.
</Note>

<Note>
  **Ember works without the full Marketer platform.** You can subscribe to Ember as a standalone product and receive creatives through the Ember dashboard independently of Marketer's broader campaign management features. When you're ready, the two connect seamlessly.
</Note>
