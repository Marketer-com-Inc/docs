Review depth should match the consequence of the result. A directional summary needs a source check. A budget or launch decision needs source, calculation, policy, and approval checks.

## Trace the result

Start with four questions:

1. **What did Marketer read?** Identify the integration, account, entity level, filters, and date range.
2. **What did Marketer calculate?** Recreate the key total, ratio, or percentage change.
3. **What did Marketer infer?** Separate an explanation from the measured facts that support it.
4. **What does Marketer propose to do?** Identify the exact write, its state, and the control that applies.

## Review by output type

### Analysis

- Confirm the requested source, metrics, and comparable windows.
- Check that missing rows are not represented as zero.
- Recalculate the number that drives the conclusion.
- Look for plausible alternative explanations.

### Creative or campaign brief

- Confirm the product, audience, objective, and required channel format.
- Check that claims are supported by approved brand context.
- Distinguish required inputs from generated suggestions.
- Keep the artifact in draft until the owner accepts it.

### Campaign or workflow plan

- Confirm the destination account, budget, schedule, targeting, and assets.
- Compare every spend-related choice with **Guardrails**.
- Verify the output or notification destination.
- Identify whether the plan creates a draft, a paused item, or an active item.

## Use an independent verification prompt

```text
Review the previous result as a verifier. Do not add a new recommendation.
Trace each decision-driving claim to its source and date range, recalculate the
main metric, list unsupported assumptions, and state whether any write action
has run. Mark uncertainty clearly.
```

## Decide what happens next

After review, choose one explicit state:

- **Accept as analysis** — save or share the result without a write action.
- **Revise** — correct the source, scope, calculation, or creative direction.
- **Prepare for approval** — keep the proposed change pending and name the approver.
- **Approve or execute** — proceed only when the workspace policy and your role allow it.
- **Stop** — reject the work when the evidence or controls are insufficient.

<Note>
  Do not use fluent wording as a confidence signal. Confidence comes from traceable evidence, reproducible calculations, and an explicit action state.
</Note>

See [Approvals and autonomy](/essential-guidelines/approvals-and-autonomy) for the controls that govern write actions.
