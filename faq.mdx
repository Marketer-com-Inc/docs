This FAQ covers the questions that come up most often before and after signing up with Marketer. You'll find answers about how the platform works, what the Ember creative subscription includes, how onboarding is structured, which integrations are supported, how your data is handled, and who owns what. If something isn't covered here, the team is reachable directly — but most of what you need to make a decision is below.

## Marketer Platform

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Marketer?">
    Marketer is an AI operating system for marketing. It brings campaigns, attribution, creative, and lifecycle into one system and runs them autonomously — so your brand always has an always-on marketing operation working in the background, not just when someone remembers to log in.

    Traditional marketing stacks are fragmented: one tool for ads, another for email, another for attribution, another for creative. Marketer is built to replace that stack with a single system that has a unified view of your data and takes action across every channel based on what it sees.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who is Marketer built for?">
    Marketer is built for e-commerce brands. If you're selling on Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce, or a custom build, Marketer connects directly to your store.

    It works for brands at any stage — from startups finding their footing on paid social to enterprise retailers managing eight-figure ad budgets. The verticals where Marketer is most active include fashion, beauty, food and beverage, home and lifestyle, and wellness, but the platform is built for any brand selling physical or digital products online.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What makes Marketer different from an agency?">
    The difference is the operating model. An agency gives you one weekly call and a team that's also managing dozens of other clients. Marketer gives you 168 optimisations a week — one for every hour — running automatically against your actual data.

    There are no notice periods, no hourly contracts, and no six-week creative cycles. Marketer is always on, and it acts on what it sees rather than waiting for a scheduled check-in. If something in your campaigns shifts at 2am on a Sunday, Marketer responds at 2am on a Sunday.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does onboarding work?">
    Onboarding takes minutes, not weeks.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Create your account">
        Sign up and set up your Marketer workspace.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Connect your accounts">
        Link your store (Shopify, WooCommerce, or whichever platform you're on) and your ad accounts (Meta, Google, Snapchat).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Marketer reads your data">
        The platform pulls your historical performance, catalogue, and customer data to build a baseline picture of your business.
      </Step>
      <Step title="First campaigns ship">
        Marketer sets up tracking and launches your first campaigns — no manual configuration required.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Ongoing direction">
        From there, Marketer tells you what to do next and keeps acting on what it learns. You stay informed through your daily brief and approve or adjust through Slack or the dashboard.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to be on Shopify?">
    No. Shopify is the most common integration, but Marketer works with WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce, and custom-built stores. If your platform isn't on that list, speak to the team — custom integrations are available for enterprise brands.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I run Marketer from Slack?">
    Yes. Connect your Slack workspace and you'll receive your daily brief there — a summary of what's running, what's changed, and what Marketer is planning to do next. You can approve or reject agent actions directly from Slack without ever opening Ads Manager. It's the fastest way to stay in the loop if you don't want to live in a dashboard.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my data secure?">
    Your data stays yours. Marketer reads and acts on your store and ad account data to run your marketing operation, but it never transfers your data to third parties. You retain full ownership of everything — customer data, order history, performance history, all of it.

    <Info>
      If you have specific data residency or compliance requirements, reach out to the team before signing up. Enterprise-tier data handling arrangements are available.
    </Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does Marketer handle approvals — does it just do things automatically?">
    Marketer is designed to act autonomously, but it's not a black box. Every action the AI agent takes is visible in your dashboard and your daily brief. For actions that exceed thresholds you set — budget changes above a certain amount, new campaign launches, major structural changes — Marketer will surface the recommendation for your approval before executing.

    You control how much autonomy the agent has. Some brands set it to fully autonomous; others prefer to approve everything. The system works either way.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Ember Creative

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Ember?">
    Ember is Marketer's AI-powered creative subscription. It produces professional marketing creative across six formats, on a recurring subscription basis, curated by real designers who ensure every asset meets brand standards before it's delivered.

    Ember is the answer to the creative volume problem — the gap between how much creative Meta and Google's algorithms need to perform and how much a brand can realistically produce in-house or commission from an agency on a traditional timeline.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What creative formats does Ember produce?">
    One Ember subscription covers six formats:

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Ad Creatives" icon="rectangle-ad">
        Static and animated ad assets built for Meta, Google, and other paid channels
      </Card>
      <Card title="Video Ads" icon="video">
        Short-form video creative optimised for paid social placements
      </Card>
      <Card title="Product Shots" icon="camera">
        Clean, on-brand product photography for ads, PDPs, and organic content
      </Card>
      <Card title="AI UGC" icon="user">
        Creator-style content that performs like user-generated content without the coordination overhead
      </Card>
      <Card title="Web Creative" icon="globe">
        Landing page imagery, banners, and on-site visual assets
      </Card>
      <Card title="Email & SMS" icon="envelope">
        Visual creative designed for Klaviyo and other lifecycle platforms
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How fast does Ember turn around creatives?">
    Most formats are delivered within 3–7 days from brief submission. The exact turnaround depends on the format, the volume requested, and whether any revisions are needed. Ember is built for a recurring production cadence — brands on a monthly subscription receive new creative batches on a predictable schedule, not whenever the agency gets around to it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the On-Brand Guarantee?">
    Ember's On-Brand Guarantee means that every creative delivered matches your brand's visual identity — colour palette, typography, tone, and aesthetic — or it gets revised until it does. Real designers review every asset before it's delivered. You won't receive generic templates with your logo dropped in.

    <Tip>
      To get the most from the On-Brand Guarantee, share your full brand guidelines at onboarding — including any brand rules about what not to do, not just what to do. The more context Ember has, the closer the first batch will be to what you'd have produced yourself.
    </Tip>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I own the creatives Ember produces?">
    Yes. All creatives produced through Ember are yours. Full ownership, full usage rights, no licensing fees, no restrictions on where or how you use them. You can run them on any platform, include them in organic content, use them in email — whatever you need.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use Ember without the Marketer platform?">
    Yes. Ember is available as a standalone subscription. You don't need to be running Marketer's campaign management or AI agent to access Ember's creative production. Many brands start with Ember and add the full Marketer platform later; others use Ember exclusively to supplement their existing creative operation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What platforms can Ember creatives be used on?">
    Ember creatives are produced to spec for Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Google (Display, Performance Max, YouTube), Snapchat, TikTok, email platforms like Klaviyo, and your own website. If you're running on a platform not listed here, brief the team at the start of the subscription — Ember can produce to custom specs when the brief is clear.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
