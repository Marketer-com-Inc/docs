Marketer is a workspace for understanding performance, creating marketing work, and moving approved work forward. It combines connected business data with an agent you can brief in plain language.

You can begin with a question, inspect the evidence behind the answer, and decide what happens next. You stay in control of changes through workspace roles, guardrails, autonomy settings, and approval steps.

## The core loop

1. **Ground the workspace.** Connect the accounts that contain your performance, commerce, or site data. Add brand and product context where it will improve the result.
2. **Ask for an outcome.** Describe the decision, analysis, brief, or workflow you need. Include the source, date range, and constraints that matter.
3. **Review the result.** Check the source, freshness, scope, assumptions, and proposed next step.
4. **Act with control.** Keep the work as a draft, approve a pending action, or let the workspace follow its configured autonomy policy.

## What you can do

| Area | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Ask Marketer questions and add campaign, brief, creative, or product context. |
| **Campaigns** | Review campaign structure and performance, then prepare or launch campaign work. |
| **Analytics** | Explore performance, creative performance, reports, and Live view. |
| **Creatives** | Work with briefs, Studio, and creative assets. |
| **Workflow** | Set up repeatable reports, alerts, and other recurring work. |
| **Workspace** | Manage chat history, notifications, Knowledge, Skills, pending actions, and settings. |

Marketer supports Meta Ads, Google Ads, Shopify, Google Analytics 4 (GA4), and Slack as core integrations. Triple Whale is available as a Beta integration. The data available in any result depends on what your workspace has connected and successfully synced.

<Note>
  Marketer does not treat a recommendation as permission to make a change. The behavior of a write action depends on your role, guardrails, autonomy settings, and any approval step shown in the product.
</Note>

## Start with a trustworthy result

Your first task should be read-only. Ask Marketer to compare performance over two complete periods, name the data source, and report any gaps. This gives you a useful result while you learn how the workspace represents sources and freshness.
