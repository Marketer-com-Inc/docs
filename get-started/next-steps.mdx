After your first verified result, expand one capability at a time. Keep the source and approval boundary explicit as the work becomes more consequential.

## Complete the activation checklist

The workspace may show these activation items:

1. **Connect your ads account**
2. **Connect MCP**
3. **Chat with agent**
4. **Generate brief**
5. **Launch ad**

They are product milestones, not a requirement to give every workspace the same autonomy. Complete **Generate brief** and **Launch ad** only when your team is ready to review creative and spend-related work.

## Before you enable actions

1. Confirm member roles and who can approve or change workspace settings.
2. Set performance targets, spend caps, budget-change rules, and protected campaigns in **Guardrails**.
3. Review the **Autonomy** level and default creative-brief mode.
4. Keep the first campaign launch paused for review.
5. Check **Awaiting action** for work that needs a decision.

See [Approvals and autonomy](/essential-guidelines/approvals-and-autonomy) before you enable a path that can publish or spend.

## Make good work reusable

When a request produces the result you need:

- Save stable business facts in **Knowledge**.
- Turn repeatable instructions into a **Skill**.
- Use **Workflow** for scheduled or event-driven work.
- Keep one-off constraints in the task that needs them.

This keeps future tasks consistent without burying temporary assumptions in workspace-wide context.
