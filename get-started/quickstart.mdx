This quickstart takes you through one complete flow: enter the correct workspace, connect one ads source, build a launch plan from approved evidence, and verify the result. The flow stops at planning; it does not create, edit, publish, pause, or launch anything.

## Before you begin

You need:

- Access to [Marketer](https://app.marketer.com)
- Permission to connect one Meta Ads or Google Ads account
- An approved product, offer, or positioning source for the workspace

If you received a workspace invitation, sign in with the invited email address. Join that workspace instead of creating a second workspace for the same company.

## 1. Enter the workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Use Google sign-in or enter your work email and complete email verification. The email path also asks you to finish your personal profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join or create the workspace">
    Accept the invited workspace when one is available. Otherwise, enter the company name and website, then confirm the country and reporting currency. A logo is optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the setup prompts">
    Choose the role and use cases that match how you plan to work. Invite teammates or connect Slack if you are ready, or skip those choices and continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the active workspace">
    When Home opens, check the workspace name. Switch before continuing if it does not own the data and Knowledge you intend to use.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Onboarding does not include plan selection. Workspace billing, when applicable and available to your role, lives in **Settings → Plans & Billing**.
</Note>

## 2. Connect one source

You can connect an account during the onboarding **Sync in data** step or later from **Integrations**.

<Frame caption="The Integrations directory shows which providers are available and which connection needs attention.">
  <img src="/assets/product-flows/integrations-directory.png" alt="Marketer Integrations directory showing available providers and connected, reconnect-needed, and unconnected states" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Select **Integrations** in the main navigation and open **Meta Ads** or **Google Ads**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the provider">
    Continue through the provider flow with an identity that can access the intended account. Review the provider permissions before granting access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the account">
    Choose only the account or accounts that belong to this Marketer workspace. Similar account names are not enough; confirm the provider identity and account selection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the first successful state">
    Look for **Connected**, then inspect the selected resources and the sync or data-coverage state shown on the provider detail page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the provider shows **Finish setup**, complete account selection. If it shows **Reconnect needed**, repair authorization before relying on its context. A connected badge alone does not prove the requested resources or dates are available.

## 3. Submit the first request

Return to **Home**. The composer sits beneath the workspace overview.

<Frame caption="Use the Home canvas to review current context and start a bounded task.">
  <img src="/assets/product-flows/home-overview.png" alt="Marketer Home canvas with performance overview, request composer, current work, daily summaries, plans, and reports" />
</Frame>

Paste this request into **Ask anything or @ to add context**:

```text
Build a launch plan for [product or offer] around our strongest proven
differentiator. Use approved workspace Knowledge and only the connected
[Meta Ads or Google Ads] context for [provider account]. Return one positioning
direction, a focused campaign sequence, and a launch checklist. Name the
evidence you used, separate facts from assumptions, and flag missing context.
Keep everything as analysis and a plan. Do not create, edit, publish, pause,
or launch anything.
```

Enter `@` and attach the exact product, brief, campaign, or creative when its identity matters. Saved Knowledge is available as workspace context; for a one-off document, use the plus menu and select **Add files or photos**.

## 4. Follow the visible state

After you submit, Marketer can take one of these paths:

| What appears | What it means | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A response in the conversation | The request can be answered directly | Let the answer finish, then review its evidence. |
| A task with visible steps | The request needs longer or coordinated work | Keep the task open or return through its task link; watch for input requests. |
| A clarification question | A required product, audience, source, account, or output is ambiguous | Answer the missing point without widening the task. |
| A proposal with decision controls | Marketer is asking permission to run displayed production work | For this plan-only quickstart, choose **Edit** or **Dismiss** rather than approving execution. |
| A connection or data error | The source is unavailable or does not cover the request | Return to **Integrations**, resolve the named prerequisite, and retry once. |

Do not start a second copy while the first task is active. A quiet conversation can still have work running behind its task detail.

## 5. Confirm the result

<Frame caption="A completed launch-plan task shows the finished work and optional next directions.">
  <img src="/assets/product-flows/task-result.png" alt="Completed Marketer launch-plan task with positioning guidance, three finished steps, and optional follow-up actions" />
</Frame>

A successful first result includes or explicitly marks as missing:

- The intended product, offer, audience, workspace, and provider account context
- The exact evidence behind the central differentiator
- One focused positioning direction
- A campaign sequence that carries the same evidence-led promise
- A launch checklist with owners, inputs, tracking, creative, destination, budget boundary, and review steps
- Assumptions and unresolved inputs
- Confirmation that no campaign, creative, budget, or provider setting changed

Ask this follow-up if the evidence or action state is unclear:

```text
Audit this launch plan before I use it. Name the exact source behind the central
differentiator, separate facts from interpretation, list every assumption and
missing input, and confirm the product, audience, offer, workspace, and provider
account context. State exactly what has and has not changed.
```

## If the result does not complete

| Problem | Next action |
| --- | --- |
| The source or product is missing | Confirm the approved source is saved in Knowledge, attach the exact product when relevant, and retry. |
| The wrong account is used | Correct the selected provider account or name the intended account explicitly in a new request. |
| The differentiator is unsupported | Add or correct the authoritative source before generating another plan. |
| The task asks to create assets or campaigns | Keep the proposal unresolved, edit the request back to plan level, or dismiss it. |
| The result is only a generic recommendation | Ask for the positioning direction, campaign sequence, launch checklist, supporting evidence, and limitations as separate sections. |

You are finished when the plan is reviewable and you can identify its evidence, scope, limitations, and action state without guessing.
