This quickstart ends with a read-only performance comparison. It does not create, edit, or launch a campaign.

## Before you begin

You need:

- Access to [Marketer](https://app.marketer.com)
- Permission to connect at least one Meta Ads or Google Ads account
- Enough history in that account to compare two completed periods

Shopify or GA4 can add commerce or site context, but neither is required for this first result.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign in with Google or enter your work email and complete email verification.

    If you received a workspace invitation, use the invited email address and join that workspace instead of creating a duplicate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your workspace">
    Enter the company name and website. Add a logo if you have one, then confirm the country and reporting currency.

    These settings give reports a consistent business context. You can update workspace settings later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect one ads account">
    In the onboarding **Sync in data** step, connect **Meta Ads** or **Google Ads**. If you skip this step, connect later from **Integrations**.

    Complete the provider authorization and select the account or accounts Marketer should use. A connected account may continue syncing after you enter the workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish workspace setup">
    Choose the role and use cases that best match how you plan to work. You can invite teammates or connect Slack, or skip those choices for now.

    There is no plan-selection step in this setup flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask your first question">
    Open **Home** and paste this request into **Ask anything or @ to add context**:

    ```text
    For the last 7 complete days, compare spend, purchases, CPA, and ROAS
    with the previous 7 complete days. Use only my connected ads-platform
    data. Name the source and both date ranges, flag any incomplete data,
    and do not change any campaigns.
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the evidence">
    Confirm that the result names:

    - The connected source it used
    - Both date ranges
    - The account or campaign scope
    - Any missing, partial, or stale data
    - The basis for its conclusion

    If one of these is missing, ask a follow-up before you rely on the result.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## A successful first result

You are done when Marketer returns a comparison grounded in your connected data and makes its scope clear. The conclusion may be that the data is incomplete. That is still a useful result because it tells you what to fix before making a decision.

<Tip>
  Use complete days for your first comparison. Today is usually a partial period and can distort a change in spend or efficiency.
</Tip>

## If no data appears

1. Open **Integrations** and confirm that the account is connected.
2. Check whether the integration shows a sync, reconnect, or account-selection step.
3. Confirm that the selected provider account contains data for both periods.
4. Ask the same question with one source and one account named explicitly.

If the connection still cannot provide data, see [Workspace and data](/get-started/workspace-and-data) before continuing.
