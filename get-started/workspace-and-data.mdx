A workspace keeps one company's data, brand context, members, controls, and work together. Select the correct workspace before you ask a question or approve an action.

## Complete onboarding

New workspace setup follows this sequence:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Use Google sign-in or verify your email address. The email path also asks you to complete your personal profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the workspace">
    Add the company name, website, optional logo, country, and reporting currency.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sync data or skip for now">
    Connect Meta Ads, Google Ads, Shopify, or Triple Whale **(Beta)** from onboarding. You can skip data sync and connect sources later from **Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your role and use cases">
    Tell Marketer how you expect to use the workspace. This personalizes setup; it does not replace the workspace permission assigned to your membership.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring in your team">
    Invite teammates, connect Slack, or skip both choices. Marketer then opens the workspace with a welcome step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Onboarding does not ask you to select a plan. If billing applies to your workspace, manage it later from **Settings → Plans & Billing** when your role permits access.
</Note>

## Choose the sources you need

Connect a source because it answers a specific kind of question:

| Source | Best for | Important boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meta Ads | Meta campaign structure and platform-reported delivery and conversion metrics | Results reflect the selected Meta account and completed sync coverage. |
| Google Ads | Google campaign structure and platform-reported performance | You may need to choose specific accounts after authorization. |
| Shopify | Products, orders, and store performance | Store figures and ad-platform figures can use different attribution rules. |
| Google Analytics 4 | Site sessions, engagement, and visitor reporting | Select a reachable GA4 property and check the reporting period. |
| Slack | Team notifications and supported collaboration flows | Connecting Slack does not make every approval available in Slack. |
| Triple Whale **(Beta)** | An additional attribution view for Meta and Google campaigns | Always name the attribution model when you use its figures. |

See [Integrations](/integrations/overview) for provider-specific setup and troubleshooting.

## Know when a source is ready

A successful authorization is the start of the connection, not proof that every date is available. Before using a source:

- Confirm the expected account or property is selected.
- Check the connection and sync status.
- Note the latest successful sync when the product shows it.
- Ask for a small, recent range before requesting a broad analysis.
- Treat missing dates as unknown, not as zero.

## Add business context

Connected metrics explain what happened. Workspace context helps Marketer interpret why it matters.

Add durable information such as positioning, customer definitions, product facts, and operating rules to **Knowledge**. Use **Branding & Brand Profile**, **Autofills**, **Guardrails**, and **Autonomy** for the workspace defaults those settings own.

For a one-off request, attach a campaign, brief, creative, or product in the composer with `@`. The attachment narrows the current request without changing the underlying source data.

## Keep workspace settings consistent

Country, currency, timezone, and account scope affect how you interpret a result. If two systems disagree, confirm that they use the same:

- Date boundaries and timezone
- Currency and any conversion policy
- Account or property
- Attribution source and model
- Metric definition
