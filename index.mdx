Marketer is the AI-powered marketing operating system built for ecommerce brands that want performance without the overhead. It reads every ad account around the clock, acts autonomously — pausing underperformers, shifting budget, launching campaigns, cleaning keywords — and delivers a morning brief to your Slack before you've had your first coffee. Pair it with Ember, the AI creative engine staffed by real designers, and you have campaigns running and creatives shipping from a single platform.

## Get up and running in three steps

From sign-up to your first AI-managed campaign, the entire onboarding flow takes minutes, not days.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your Marketer account">
    Head to [marketer.com](https://marketer.com) and sign up. Choose the Marketer platform if you want the full AI agent experience, or start with Ember only if you need creatives first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your store and ad accounts">
    Link your ecommerce store — Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce, or a custom build — along with your Meta, Google, Snapchat, and ChatGPT Ads accounts. Marketer reads your product catalog, order history, campaign performance, and audience data to build a complete picture of your business.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let Marketer run">
    Marketer indexes your data, surfaces the first campaign brief, and starts optimizing immediately. Approve actions from Slack, review the morning brief, and watch performance data feed back into every decision the agent makes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Ember is available as a standalone product.** You don't need the full Marketer platform to access studio-quality ad creatives, video ads, product shots, AI UGC, and email creative. [Learn more about Ember](/ember/overview).
</Note>
