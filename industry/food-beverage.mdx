Food and beverage brands live and die by angle selection. Whether you're launching a functional soda, a protein bar, or a supplement-adjacent snack, the same product can land entirely differently depending on whether you lead with ingredients, lifestyle context, energy and performance, or taste and occasion. Most brands test one angle at a time and wait weeks for a verdict. Marketer runs the whole test matrix at once — and acts on the results overnight.

## How Marketer Solves It

Marketer's AI Agent doesn't wait for your weekly performance review. It watches your campaigns through the night and acts when performance signals shift — pausing underperforming ad sets, moving budget toward the angles that are working, cleaning up keyword waste, and queuing new ads so that by the time you check in the next morning, the account has already been optimized. A protein bar brand running Marketer might wake up to find the Agent has paused cold-prospecting ad sets with falling ROAS, shifted budget to the energy angle that was outperforming, and pushed four new ads live — all before 9am.

Ember generates the creative volume that makes angle-testing real. Product-focused shots, lifestyle contexts, and ingredient or formula close-ups are produced in the batches you need to actually run a multivariate test — not just two ads, but enough variants to find the angle that wins with each audience segment. When a winning angle emerges, the performance loop feeds that signal back into the next creative brief, so your next batch is already optimized before you brief it.

Klaviyo connects the paid-media story to your owned channels. Subscription flows, replenishment reminders, and launch emails run from the same performance data as your ad campaigns — so a customer who just converted on your energy angle gets a replenishment sequence that speaks the same language, not a generic welcome series.
