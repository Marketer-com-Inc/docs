Home and lifestyle brands face a creative problem that most platforms aren't built to solve: you don't have one hero product — you have a catalogue. Every SKU needs a product shot. Every collection needs a lifestyle context. Every channel needs a format that fits. When you're managing hundreds of SKUs across Meta and Google, the creative production bottleneck isn't strategy — it's volume. Marketer removes that bottleneck by producing creative coverage at the scale your catalogue actually demands.

## How Marketer Solves It

Ember generates product and lifestyle creative at SKU level, which means you're not choosing which products get advertising support and which don't — your full catalogue gets coverage. Whether you're running serveware, bags, furniture, or a broad lifestyle range, Ember produces the clean product shots and aspirational lifestyle contexts needed to compete on paid social at the volume a large catalogue requires. Brands like SoftMoc have used Marketer to produce 536 creatives in 10 weeks while managing $250K+ in monthly Meta spend — the kind of output that simply isn't achievable through a traditional agency or in-house creative team alone.

Marketer's AI Agent manages spend performance across that creative volume in real time. With hundreds of ads in market simultaneously, manual optimization isn't practical — the Agent monitors every active campaign, catches budget leaks and underperforming placements before they compound, and surfaces the product-level and angle-level signals that tell you where to concentrate spend. You get the creative coverage of a large catalogue with the optimization discipline of a performance-focused team.

On the channel side, Meta and Google run from a single source of truth, so the creative that's working on one channel informs the strategy on the other. The performance loop closes the cycle — live campaign data routes back into the next Ember creative brief, ensuring that your catalogue coverage gets smarter with every batch rather than simply bigger.
