Wellness brands sell transformation — but the angle through which you tell that story makes all the difference between a scroll-stop and a skip. The same supplement brand can see radically different ROAS depending on whether you lead with ingredients and science, a founder's personal journey, before-and-after results, or an aspirational lifestyle. Most brands settle on one angle and stick with it. The ones growing fastest are running all four simultaneously, reading the signal in real time, and concentrating spend on what converts before creative fatigue sets in.

## How Marketer Solves It

Ember generates creative across every angle your wellness brand needs: ingredient and formula close-ups that speak to efficacy, before-and-after sequences that show real outcomes, founder story formats that build trust and brand affinity, and lifestyle contexts that anchor the product in how your customer wants to feel. Each angle is produced in the volume needed to run a genuine multivariate test — not one ad per concept, but a batch that gives the algorithm enough signal to surface a winner quickly.

Marketer's AI Agent watches your campaigns for the two performance problems that quietly erode wellness advertising: creative fatigue and budget misallocation. Supplement audiences in particular can tire of creative fast, especially on Facebook and Instagram where the same face or format becomes invisible through repetition. The Agent catches the early signs — declining click-through, rising CPM, stalling ROAS — and flags rotation before performance deteriorates. When an ingredient angle is outperforming a lifestyle angle, the Agent surfaces that signal and recommends budget concentration, so you're not splitting spend equally across angles that aren't performing equally.

Klaviyo replenishment flows close the loop on the customer journey. For supplement brands, replenishment timing is everything — a customer who finishes their first bottle and doesn't immediately reorder is at high risk of churning to a competitor. Klaviyo flows running from the same Marketer source of truth send the right replenishment message at the right moment, with creative and copy that matches the angle that originally converted each customer.
