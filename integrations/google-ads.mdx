Connect Google Ads to bring selected advertising accounts into Marketer's blended reporting and analysis.

## Prerequisites

- A Marketer Owner, Admin, or Member role
- A Google identity that can reach the required Google Ads accounts
- The customer IDs of the leaf accounts you want to sync
- Agreement on the workspace reporting currency and date range you will use for validation

## Connect Google Ads

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Google Ads">
    Go to **Integrations**, then open **Google Ads**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize read access">
    Select **Connect Google Ads** and complete Google's consent flow. Review the access Google displays.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose accounts">
    Select the non-manager accounts that should enter this workspace's blended reporting. A manager account can expose its children, but Marketer syncs the leaf accounts you select.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the connection">
    Check for **Connected**, the Google identity, the number of selected accounts, and the connected date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Confirm the first sync

Wait for the sync state to complete, then review **Synced Google Ads accounts** and the data-coverage view. Each selected account should show its customer ID and currency.

As a first validation, ask which campaigns used the most spend over a completed date range. Compare the account, currency, and totals with Google Ads before using the answer for a decision.

## Data and permission boundaries

Marketer imports campaigns, ad groups, ads, and performance from the leaf accounts you select. The connection page is explicit about read access; any additional product action remains subject to the action's own permissions, your workspace role, and workspace policy.

Account selection is workspace-specific. A Viewer can inspect the selected accounts but cannot change the selection. Optional Google recommendation controls, when shown, are separate from the connection itself.

## Freshness and reporting

Use sync state and coverage to judge whether a period is ready for analysis. Recent Google Ads metrics can change as conversions and attribution settle.

When a selected account uses a different currency, blended views convert reporting to the workspace reporting currency. Confirm the source currency before comparing raw Google Ads values with a blended Marketer view.

## Reconnect, resync, or disconnect

- Choose **Manage accounts** to add or remove leaf accounts while authorization remains active.
- Choose **Refresh account access** when authorization exists but no selectable accounts were saved.
- Choose **Reconnect Google Ads** when Google invalidates the authorization.
- If an account shows **Access lost**, restore access in Google Ads or remove that account from the selection. A normal resync cannot repair a provider-side denial.
- If status is unavailable, refresh before changing the connection. Marketer preserves the last-known state while verification fails.
- Disconnect only after checking which reports and workflows depend on the selected accounts.
