Connecting Google Ads to Marketer gives your AI agent a complete read of your search and shopping activity — campaigns, ad groups, keywords, search term reports, and performance at every level. From there, the agent works continuously: flagging budget inefficiencies, identifying keywords that are eating spend without converting, and building the briefs that turn insights into action. Work that used to require an analyst pulling reports and a specialist making account changes happens automatically, overnight, so you start each morning with a cleaner account and a clearer picture.

## How to Connect Google Ads

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Integrations">
    In Marketer, navigate to **Settings** in the left sidebar and select **Integrations** from the menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Connect Google Ads'">
    Find the Google Ads card in your integrations list and click **Connect Google Ads** to begin the authorisation flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with Google and grant access">
    You'll be redirected to Google. Sign in with the Google account that has admin access to your Google Ads account and grant Marketer the requested permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your Google Ads accounts">
    After granting access, select which Google Ads accounts to connect. If you manage a manager account (MCC), you can connect multiple child accounts from a single authorisation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Marketer indexes your account">
    Marketer begins indexing your campaigns, ad groups, keywords, search terms, and performance data. Once indexing is complete, your agent can start surfacing insights and queuing actions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the Agent Can Do on Google Ads

Once connected, your Marketer agent can take the following actions — each surfaced for your approval before executing:

- **Shift budget** between campaigns and ad groups based on performance data
- **Clean negative keywords** — identify and add search terms that are converting poorly and wasting spend
- **Launch new ad groups** built from campaign briefs and product or category data
- **Pause underperformers** — ad groups, keywords, or campaigns that are draining budget without results
- **Build new responsive search ads** — headline and description variants generated from your briefs

<Note>
  As with all connected channels, Marketer surfaces every material action for your approval before making changes to your Google Ads account. Budget shifts, keyword additions, pauses, and launches all appear in your approval queue. You can configure thresholds for routine maintenance tasks — like small negative keyword additions — to auto-approve if you prefer.
</Note>

<Tip>
  One of the most consistent overnight wins Marketer delivers is keyword hygiene. A typical entry in the activity log reads: **"06:45 — Cleaned 12 negative keywords across 3 campaigns."** Over weeks, this quietly compounds — less wasted spend, higher quality scores, and search term reports that actually mean something. Connect Google Ads early and let the agent start building that clean foundation from day one.
</Tip>
