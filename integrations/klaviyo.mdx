Your ad data and your email and SMS flows are describing the same customers — but if they live in separate tools, they're telling different stories. Connecting Klaviyo to Marketer puts both on the same source of truth: the same customer segments that inform your Meta audiences also trigger your lifecycle flows, and the revenue Klaviyo attributes shows up in the same dashboard as your paid spend. The result is attribution that actually holds up, audiences built from real purchase behaviour, and a lifecycle programme that reacts to what's happening in your store — not what happened a week ago in a separate report.

## How to Connect Klaviyo

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Integrations">
    In Marketer, navigate to **Settings** in the left sidebar and select **Integrations** from the menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Connect Klaviyo'">
    Find the Klaviyo card in your integrations list and click **Connect Klaviyo**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your Klaviyo API key">
    Marketer connects to Klaviyo using a private API key. To find yours, go to **Klaviyo → Settings → API Keys**, create a new private key with full access, and paste it into Marketer. Keep this key secure — treat it like a password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Marketer syncs your Klaviyo data">
    Once your API key is validated, Marketer syncs your Klaviyo lists, segments, and flow performance data. Your Klaviyo-attributed revenue is included in the unified Marketer dashboard from this point forward.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Marketer Does with Your Klaviyo Data

### Audience Segmentation for Paid Channels

Marketer uses your Klaviyo customer segments to build and refresh ad audiences across Meta and other connected channels. Instead of manually exporting lists, the agent keeps your custom audiences current — automatically syncing segments like:

- **Lapsed buyers** — customers who haven't purchased in 60, 90, or 120 days, ready for win-back campaigns
- **High-LTV customers** — your best buyers, used for lookalike seed audiences on Meta
- **New-to-brand** — recent first-purchase customers for onboarding flows and cross-sell campaigns

### Lifecycle Flows Triggered by Real Signals

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Replenishment Flows" icon="arrows-rotate">
    For products with predictable repurchase cycles, Marketer triggers replenishment emails and SMS at the right moment — based on purchase data from your connected store, not a fixed schedule.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Drop Announcements" icon="tag">
    New product launches and limited drops fire from the same source of truth as your ad campaigns. Your email list and your paid audiences hear about a new drop at the same moment, from the same brief.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Attribution in One Place

Klaviyo-attributed revenue is pulled into Marketer's unified dashboard alongside your paid channel data. You see total revenue, broken down by source — paid social, search, email, and SMS — without reconciling separate reports.

<Info>
  Before connecting Klaviyo, most brands run three dashboards: Meta Ads Manager, Google Ads, and Klaviyo — each with its own attribution model and each claiming a share of the same conversion. Marketer replaces that with one number you can actually defend. Klaviyo revenue and paid revenue are measured against the same order data from your store, so the total adds up correctly.
</Info>

<Tip>
  If you run a beauty or fashion brand with regular product drops, this integration delivers its biggest return on drop day. Your paid audiences on Meta and your Klaviyo email and SMS lists fire from the same announcement brief — same timing, same message, same creative direction. Replenishment flows for consumable products (skincare, supplements, beauty staples) also improve significantly once Klaviyo and your store data are on the same source of truth, because the trigger is based on actual purchase behaviour rather than estimated repurchase windows.
</Tip>
