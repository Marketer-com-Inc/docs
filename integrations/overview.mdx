Real attribution starts when your ad spend, store data, and lifecycle data stop living in separate tools. Marketer pulls Meta, Google, Shopify, and Klaviyo into one operating layer so your AI agent always works from a single, consistent picture of performance — not a patchwork of dashboards that each tell a different story. When a campaign brief is written, a budget is shifted, or a flow is triggered, every decision is grounded in the same numbers you'd defend to your board.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Meta" icon="meta" href="/integrations/meta">
    Connect your Meta Business account to give Marketer access to campaigns, ad sets, creatives, audiences, and pixel events. The agent reads, recommends, and acts — with your approval.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Google Ads" icon="google" href="/integrations/google">
    Link Google Ads so Marketer can manage campaigns, clean negative keywords, shift budgets, and surface performance insights — all without manual Ads Manager work.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Klaviyo" icon="envelope" href="/integrations/klaviyo">
    Put your email and SMS flows on the same source of truth as your ad data. Smarter segmentation, real attribution, and lifecycle triggers that actually reflect what's happening in your store.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Shopify" icon="bag-shopping" href="/integrations/shopify">
    Connect your Shopify store so Marketer uses real product, order, and revenue data to run campaigns and report accurate ROAS — not modelled estimates.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What Each Integration Provides

<Accordion title="Data each integration sends to Marketer">

| Integration | Data Marketer Reads |
|---|---|
| **Meta** | Campaigns, ad sets, creatives, audiences, pixel events, performance metrics |
| **Google Ads** | Campaigns, ad groups, keywords, search terms, performance metrics |
| **Klaviyo** | Lists, segments, flow performance, email/SMS attributed revenue |
| **Shopify** | Product catalog, pricing, inventory, orders, revenue, customer purchase history |
| **Snapchat Ads** | Campaign structure, spend, performance data |
| **ChatGPT Ads** | Campaign data, performance metrics |

</Accordion>

Marketer also supports store connections beyond Shopify — including WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce, and custom-built storefronts. See the [Shopify integration page](/integrations/shopify) for details on connecting non-Shopify stores.

<Note>
  Your data stays yours. Marketer reads your connected accounts to surface insights and power your AI agent — it does not transfer, sell, or store your data outside of the permissions you grant. You can disconnect any integration at any time from Settings → Integrations.
</Note>
