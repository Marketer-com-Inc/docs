Your ad campaigns are only as smart as the store data behind them. When Marketer connects to your Shopify store, your AI agent gains a live read of your full product catalog, order history, revenue, and customer purchase data — the same numbers your finance team looks at. That means campaign briefs are informed by what's actually in stock and what's selling, ROAS is calculated against real order revenue rather than modelled estimates, and audience segments are built from verified purchase behaviour. Shopify is one of Marketer's earliest and most deeply integrated ecommerce connections, and connecting it is the fastest way to move from ad-platform attribution to true business attribution.

## How to Connect Shopify

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Integrations">
    In Marketer, navigate to **Settings** in the left sidebar and select **Integrations** from the menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Connect Shopify'">
    Find the Shopify card in your integrations list and click **Connect Shopify**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your Shopify store URL">
    Enter your store URL in the format `yourstore.myshopify.com`. This identifies your store in the Shopify ecosystem.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the Marketer app from the Shopify App Store">
    You'll be redirected to the Shopify App Store to install the Marketer app. Review the permissions and click **Install**. This grants Marketer secure read access to your store data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Marketer syncs your store data">
    Once installed, Marketer syncs your product catalog, orders, revenue, and customer data. Depending on your store's order history, the initial sync may take a few minutes. After that, data stays current in real time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How Marketer Uses Your Shopify Data

**Campaign briefs grounded in product data.** When you ask Marketer to build a campaign, it references your live catalog — current pricing, available inventory, and product details — so briefs are accurate and creatives reflect what customers will actually see when they land on your site.

**ROAS calculated against real revenue.** Instead of relying on ad-platform attribution models, Marketer calculates return on ad spend against verified Shopify order data. The number you see is the number that matches your bank account.

**Audience segments built from purchase behaviour.** High-LTV customers, lapsed buyers, first-time purchasers, and product-specific buyers are all segmented from your actual order data and synced to your connected ad platforms — giving Meta and Google audiences that reflect real purchase signals.

<Note>
  Marketer also supports stores built on **WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce**, and **custom-built platforms**. If you're not on Shopify, you can still connect your store and give your agent access to the same product, order, and revenue data. See the [Integrations Overview](/integrations/overview) page for the full list of supported platforms and how to get started.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Once your catalog is connected, Marketer's creative engine **Ember** can produce ad creative for every product in your store — not just hero SKUs. SoftMoc, a footwear retailer with a catalog of over 3,000 SKUs, used Ember to generate 536 distinct creatives mapped to specific products. That kind of coverage isn't possible with a traditional creative team and a manual production process — but it's exactly what a connected catalog makes possible.
</Tip>
