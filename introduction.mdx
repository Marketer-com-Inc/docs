Running paid advertising across multiple channels used to mean juggling agencies, freelancers, bid management tools, creative studios, and attribution platforms — each with their own logins, invoices, and lag time. Marketer replaces that entire stack with a single AI operating system that monitors every ad account 24/7, takes action without waiting for a weekly call, and ties campaign performance directly to creative production so every brief is smarter than the last.

## Core products

### Marketer — the AI agent

The Marketer agent reads your ad accounts continuously and acts in real time. It pauses underperforming ad sets, reallocates budget toward what's working, launches new campaigns, and scrubs wasted keyword spend — all without you needing to log in. Every morning it sends a brief to Slack summarising overnight activity and flagging decisions that need your approval. You can approve or reject any action directly from Slack, keeping you in control without keeping you in a dashboard.

The agent runs across **Meta**, **Google Ads**, **Snapchat**, and **ChatGPT Ads**, and connects to your ecommerce store to ground every decision in real revenue data.

### Ember — the AI creative engine

Ember is the creative arm of the Marketer platform. Brief a new creative inside the platform, and a combination of real designers and AI produces it — static ads, video ads, product shots, AI UGC, web creative, and email and SMS assets. Because Ember lives inside the same platform as the agent, performance data feeds automatically back into the next brief. If a hook isn't converting, the next video already knows. Clients own every creative that ships, and Ember's On-Brand Guarantee means quality is never a question.

Ember can be used as a standalone subscription if you need creatives without the full AI agent.
