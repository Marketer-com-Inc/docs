**Creatives** is Marketer's creative-performance surface. Use it to compare delivered Meta creative under one explicit date and filter frame—not to browse every saved design in the workspace.

Open [Creatives](https://app.marketer.com/dashboard/creatives).

## Know the data boundary

- The current performance rows come from synced Meta creative delivery.
- Studio-only concepts and manual uploads have no delivery metrics until they are used in a synced Meta ad.
- **Creative Assets** owns saved and synced files; **Creatives** owns the performance comparison.
- Date controls scope the metrics. They do not prove when the underlying file was created.

If Meta is disconnected, the surface can show **Creative data isn't synced yet**. During first import it can show **Setting up your creatives…**; a failed or incomplete import can show **Creative sync needs attention**.

## Establish a comparable set

Work from broad scope to narrow scope:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the complete date window">
    Choose the same complete dates used in the campaign diagnosis. Keep the window fixed while comparing rows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set delivery status">
    Choose **All status**, **Active**, or **Paused**. Status describes the linked delivery object, not whether the asset file remains in the library.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the creative format">
    Choose **All formats**, **Image**, **Video**, or **Carousel**. Compare formats together only when the decision genuinely spans them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set ratio and ownership">
    Use **All ratios** or the available aspect ratio. Narrow to **All campaigns** or one campaign and **All ad sets** or one ad set when the audience, objective, or placement must be held constant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Constrain delivery volume">
    Use the **Spend range** and **Days live range** controls to avoid calling a low-delivery outlier a winner. Keep minimum evidence requirements consistent across candidates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the result metric and order">
    Select the metric pill that answers the question, then set **Sort**. A ranking is only meaningful under the current filters and window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find and identify the row">
    Use **Search creative name…** and switch between grid and table view. Similar thumbnails or names do not prove two rows are the same creative.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Decide when to use Split by ad

By default, Marketer can group delivery around the creative. Turn on **Split by ad** when you need one row per ad using that creative.

Use the grouped view when asking, “How did this creative perform overall?” Use **Split by ad** when asking, “Did the same creative behave differently in separate ads?”

<Warning>
  Switching to ad-level rows changes the grain. Do not compare a grouped creative row with an ad-level row as if they were peers, and do not add both together.
</Warning>

Campaign and ad-set filters apply to the corresponding delivery relationships. If a creative has run in several campaigns, narrowing one parent can materially change its result.

## Read the comparison

For every candidate:

1. Confirm its spend and days live are sufficient for the decision.
2. Read the outcome metric and its efficiency measure together.
3. Compare candidates under compatible campaign objective, audience, placement, format, and delivery volume.
4. Look for consistency, not one extreme row.
5. Open the detail before carrying the learning forward.

An image with high ROAS on very little spend is a lead for investigation. It is not automatically a reusable rule.

## Inspect creative detail

Open a row to inspect the selected creative without losing the comparison frame.

The detail can include:

- A time-series chart for the selected metric
- Performance totals for the current date window
- **Age**, **Gender**, **Country**, **Platform**, and **Audience** breakdown tabs when data is available
- **Where live** placement and delivery context
- Identifying labels and metadata
- Dynamic-creative or delivery-diagnostic detail when the linked ad supports it

Use the breakdown tabs to test a specific explanation. For example, if one creative appears stronger, check whether that difference is concentrated in one country, platform, or audience rather than general across delivery.

### Confirm the asset identity

Before reuse, verify:

- Visual and format
- Creative name and linked ad identity
- Campaign and ad set under which it served
- Whether a resized or dynamic variant is represented
- Current status and where it remains live

Continue to [Creative Assets](/product-guides/creatives-assets) when you need the reusable file, copy, usage roster, or per-ad status controls.

## Turn evidence into a new direction

Carry a creative learning into [Briefs](/product-guides/creatives-briefs) as a hypothesis with evidence:

```text
In Meta prospecting, 2026-07-01 through 2026-07-14, square product-in-use
images with a proof-led hook produced the strongest qualified result among
ads above our minimum spend. Test whether the proof-led hook or the product-in-use
composition explains the change; keep the audience, offer, and destination fixed.
```

This preserves the conditions under which the result appeared. Avoid converting a single creative result into a permanent Brand Profile fact or Skill until it repeats under comparable conditions.

## Completion check

- Meta is connected and its creative sync covers the selected dates.
- The date, status, format, ratio, campaign, ad set, spend, and days-live filters are recorded.
- Every comparison uses the same row grain.
- Candidates have compatible delivery conditions and sufficient evidence.
- The detail view confirms the creative identity and where it served.
- Breakdowns were used to test, not merely decorate, the explanation.
- The resulting brief states the observed fact, hypothesis, controlled variables, and next test separately.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Setting up your creatives does not finish">
    Open Meta in Integrations and inspect authorization, selected ad account, and sync coverage. Return after the import completes; do not treat placeholders as zero delivery.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Creative sync needs attention">
    Repair the named Meta connection or sync issue, then retry the affected source. Keep the original date frame so the refreshed result can be compared directly.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A known creative is missing">
    Reset status, format, ratio, campaign, ad set, spend, days-live, search, and date filters. Confirm the creative actually delivered from the connected Meta account in that window.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Grouped and ad-level numbers differ">
    Check **Split by ad**. The grouped row can represent delivery across several ads; the split view shows each ad separately. Choose one grain for the question.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The detail shows an unexpected audience result">
    Confirm the date window and parent campaign/ad set, then inspect the relevant **Audience**, **Country**, **Platform**, **Age**, or **Gender** tab. A concentrated segment can explain the aggregate without applying to every viewer.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
