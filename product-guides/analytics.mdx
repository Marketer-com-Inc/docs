**Analytics** combines connected marketing and commerce data into a reviewable performance surface. The core workflow is: define the frame, find the change, inspect the object that owns it, and preserve the evidence with the decision.

Open [Analytics](https://app.marketer.com/dashboard/analytics?view=performance&channel=all) and confirm the active workspace.

## Before you begin

- Connect the source that owns the question: Meta, Google Ads, **Microsoft Ads (Beta)**, Google Analytics, Shopify, or **OpenAI Ads (Beta)**.
- Let the required date range finish syncing.
- Confirm the workspace reporting timezone and display currency.
- Use **Triple Whale (Beta)** only when you intentionally need its eligible attribution view; record its attribution model.

## Choose the owning surface

| Question | Start in | Leave with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What changed across channels or commerce? | **Analytics → Performance** | A measured change and the leading breakdown |
| Which delivered creative is responsible? | **Creatives** | A like-for-like Meta creative comparison |
| Should this review recur? | **Reports** under Analytics | A configured report with a verified run and delivery |
| What file, campaign, or product owns the result? | Creative Assets, Campaigns, or Products | The exact source record and current state |

## Follow the measurement lifecycle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Define the frame">
    Set complete dates, **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly**, or **Yearly** resolution, comparison, channel, included accounts, currency, and attribution. Use **Manage metrics → Visible metrics** to keep the relevant cards in view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read outcome, spend, and trend">
    Start with the business result, read spend beside it, then locate when the change began. Do not infer cause from one correlated chart.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inspect the contributing grain">
    Move through the available campaign, search, landing-page, product, country, device, or other breakdown, then open the owning object.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record and operationalize">
    Separate observed fact, hypothesis, and next check. Save a reusable custom view or report only after the frame is correct.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Important boundaries

- A syncing placeholder, withheld card, or missing value is not zero.
- **All channels** is complete only for the accounts and sources shown by **Accounts**.
- Switching to **Triple Whale (Beta)** creates a new attribution frame.
- The custom-view builder can support fewer channels than the main Analytics control.
- The current creative-performance surface is [Creatives](https://app.marketer.com/dashboard/creatives).
- A successful report data run and successful Slack/email delivery are separate outcomes.

## Completion check

- Workspace, account, complete dates, timezone, currency, channel, resolution, comparison, and attribution are recorded.
- Every decision-driving source has usable coverage or an explicit limitation.
- Outcome and spend were read before ratios and breakdowns.
- The campaign, creative, product, or integration that owns the result was verified.
- Fact, hypothesis, and next action remain distinct.
- Any saved view or report reproduces the intended frame.

## If the result looks wrong

Reset to a recent complete window, confirm **Accounts**, clear filters, and align provider, timezone, currency, attribution, conversion definition, and object grain. Open Integrations when a source remains syncing or disconnected. Preserve both frames when a provider discrepancy remains rather than forcing the values to match.
