**Creatives** brings planning, production, and the asset library into one product area. It has three primary surfaces: **Briefs**, **Studio**, and **Assets**.

Use the full flow when you need a traceable path from a marketing goal to a reviewed asset. You can also enter at the surface that matches work already in progress.

## Plan with Briefs

A strong brief prevents generated work from filling important gaps with assumptions. Include:

- The business objective and campaign context.
- The audience, insight, and desired response.
- The product or offer, including facts that must remain exact.
- The angle, hook, and message hierarchy.
- The required proof and prohibited claims.
- Brand voice and visual constraints.
- Intended placement or format.
- The criteria a reviewer will use to accept the result.

Briefs have their own lifecycle. Treat a brief as planning context until its scope and evidence are approved. A finished brief is not a launched campaign or a completed asset by itself.

## Create in Studio

Studio uses the context you provide to create and refine creative work. Depending on the current workspace capabilities, you can start from a prompt, attach product or reference context, or open Studio from another Marketer surface.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the source of truth">
    Start with an approved brief or a clearly defined prompt. Attach the relevant Shopify-backed product when exact product identity matters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define the creative direction">
    State the audience insight, angle, hook, desired format, and brand constraints. Distinguish inspiration from material that must be copied exactly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate a focused set">
    Ask for distinct options with an explicit difference between them. A smaller, intentional set is easier to compare than many near-duplicates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and refine">
    Inspect product facts, claims, brand voice, visual accuracy, legibility, and placement fit. Give specific revision instructions tied to the acceptance criteria.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move approved work forward">
    Keep the finished material in Assets and use it in a campaign flow only after the creative and campaign context are both reviewed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Work with Assets

**Assets** is the workspace library for creative material. Use its search and filters to locate the right item, open its detail, and confirm what you are reusing.

Before you place an existing asset into new work, check:

- The product, offer, and price are still current.
- The claim and proof are valid for the new audience and placement.
- The format meets the destination requirements.
- The asset has enough delivery or performance evidence for the conclusion you are drawing.
- The usage fits current brand and policy guidance.

## Common outcomes

### Turn a product into a brief

Attach the product in chat or start from Products. Ask Marketer to use current brand Knowledge and return a brief with multiple distinct angles, required proof, and review criteria. Review the brief before opening production.

### Create a test slate

Use one approved brief and vary a meaningful creative dimension, such as the angle or hook. Keep the offer and audience stable when you want the performance result to teach you about that dimension.

### Learn from performance

Open **Creative performance** in Analytics with a defined date and channel context. Compare assets with sufficient delivery, then carry the supported learning into the next brief. Do not turn one high-performing result into a universal creative rule without enough evidence.

### Reuse an existing asset

Find it in Assets, inspect its current details, and confirm that the new campaign or placement uses compatible product facts and requirements. Reuse should be a deliberate decision, not just the shortest path to launch.

## Creative review checklist

Review every customer-facing output for:

1. Product identity, features, price, and availability.
2. Claims and the evidence required to support them.
3. Brand voice, visual identity, and prohibited language.
4. Audience fit and message clarity.
5. Placement, dimensions, legibility, and safe areas.
6. Consistency between the asset and its destination.
7. Required human, legal, or policy review.

<Warning>
  Generated creative can look complete while containing an invented detail. Verify product facts and claims against approved workspace sources before you publish or attach the asset to a campaign.
</Warning>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The output is off-brand">
    Confirm the Brand Profile and relevant Knowledge are current, then restate the voice and visual constraints in the brief. Give the Studio a concrete correction instead of asking for a generic improvement.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The product is wrong or missing">
    Confirm the Shopify connection and product sync, then attach the exact product record. Do not rely on a typed product name when similar variants exist.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The options look too similar">
    Assign a different audience insight, angle, or visual treatment to each option. Keep the business objective stable so the set remains comparable.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A new item is not visible in Assets">
    Confirm that creation finished successfully, clear filters, and check the expected format. Open the originating task or Studio result if the generation is still active or failed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Creative performance is inconclusive">
    Check delivery volume, date range, channel, attribution view, and whether the assets were served under comparable conditions. Record uncertainty instead of forcing a winner.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
