**Knowledge** and **Skills** solve different problems:

- Knowledge holds approved source material, brand facts, reference context, and grounded artifacts.
- Skills holds reusable working procedures and the conditions under which agents should use them.

Open [Knowledge](https://app.marketer.com/dashboard/knowledge-base) or [Skills](https://app.marketer.com/dashboard/knowledge-base/skills) and confirm the active workspace.

## Decide what you are adding

| Material | Route | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Approved brand identity | **Brand kit material** | Voice, visual rules, enduring positioning |
| Facts and reference evidence | **Reference knowledge** | Product proof, research, audience source material |
| Reusable method | **Working procedure** | Launch QA, naming method, review checklist |
| Mixed or unclear source | **Let the agent decide** | Use only when you will inspect the resulting route |

Put changing performance numbers in Analytics or their owning surface. Put a repeatable method for evaluating those numbers in a Skill.

## Follow the context lifecycle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Establish authority">
    Confirm source owner, effective date, approved use, and whether a newer source conflicts. Remove secrets and personal data the workspace should not retain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build or upload">
    Use **Build from website** for the canonical public site, or route approved files through Sources. Wait for a terminal state and verify the resulting Brand kit, Knowledge, or Skill badge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inspect grounding">
    Review the **Standing index**, open **Recent artifacts**, and inspect the sources named by **Grounded in N**. Distinguish sourced fact from synthesis and recommendation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Govern procedures">
    Review Brand foundation, active Skills, **Ready to promote**, proposed and archived items, and learning evidence. Read every imported procedure before activation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify in a fresh task">
    Test one fact or method in a new task, require the named source or procedure, then run a nearby negative test that should not trigger the Skill.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Important boundaries

- The **Standing index** is compact context, not the entire knowledge base.
- A website build reflects the site when research ran; rebuild after material changes.
- Moving a source out of Brand kit does not guarantee previously extracted brand details disappear.
- Images and fonts behave as brand assets rather than ordinary factual documents.
- **Brand foundation** is always on and is not deleted like an ordinary Skill.
- Imported Skills become active after import, so the preview is an approval boundary.
- Usage and win rate are review signals; **Outcomes pending** is not 0% performance.

## Completion check

- Every important source has an owner, date, authority, and intentional route.
- Website and file work reached a terminal state.
- Artifact claims can be traced to the named sources.
- Active Skills have a specific trigger, scope, steps, branches, approvals, stop conditions, and final check.
- Imported or promoted Skills were reviewed for overlap, secrets, unsupported authority, and stale assumptions.
- Fresh positive and negative tests confirmed retrieval and Skill scope.

## If context is wrong

Check source state, route, effective date, and conflicts before editing the prompt. Replace stale evidence, reroute incorrect material, and retest in a new task. When a Skill is ignored or overused, correct **When to use**, exclusions, scope, or status rather than duplicating the procedure.
