By the end of this guide your ecommerce store, ad accounts, and Slack workspace will all be connected to Marketer, and the AI agent will have read your data, prepared your first campaign brief, and be ready to start optimizing. The whole process takes minutes — most brands have their first campaigns live within the hour.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your Marketer account">
    Go to [marketer.com](https://marketer.com) and sign up for an account. During sign-up you'll choose your plan:

    - **Marketer platform** — the full AI agent plus Ember creative, covering campaigns, attribution, creative, and lifecycle in one system.
    - **Ember only** — standalone access to the Ember creative engine if you want studio-quality creatives without the full agent.

    You can upgrade or switch plans at any time from your account settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your store">
    Link your ecommerce store so Marketer has a live view of your product catalog, pricing, and order data. This grounds every campaign decision in real revenue performance rather than ad-platform metrics alone.

    Marketer supports:
    - **Shopify** — connect via the Marketer app in the Shopify App Store
    - **WooCommerce** — install the Marketer plugin and authenticate
    - **Magento** — connect via API credentials in your Marketer dashboard
    - **BigCommerce** — connect via the BigCommerce app directory
    - **Custom storefronts** — connect using the Marketer REST API or webhook integration

    Once connected, Marketer syncs your products, orders, revenue, and customer data and uses it to inform every campaign it builds and every budget decision it makes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your ad accounts">
    Connect each ad platform you run. Marketer reads your existing campaigns, creatives, audiences, and historical performance data so it can optimise from day one rather than starting cold.

    Supported ad channels:
    - **Meta** (Facebook & Instagram Ads)
    - **Google Ads**
    - **Snapchat Ads**
    - **ChatGPT Ads**

    For each channel, you'll grant Marketer read and write access to your ad account through the platform's standard OAuth or API key flow. See [Connecting Channels](/connecting-channels) for the step-by-step instructions for each platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Slack (optional but recommended)">
    Link your Slack workspace to receive the daily morning brief and manage agent actions without opening a dashboard. Once connected, Marketer posts overnight activity summaries and surfaces any decisions that need your approval directly in a Slack channel of your choice.

    You can approve or reject individual agent actions — a budget shift, a paused ad set, a new campaign launch — with a single click inside Slack. See [Slack Integration](/agent/slack-integration) for setup details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your first brief">
    After your accounts are connected, Marketer reads all your data and prepares the first campaign brief. It surfaces the campaigns it recommends launching, the audiences it plans to target, and the budget allocations it suggests — then asks for your approval before taking action.

    Review the brief in your dashboard or in Slack, approve what looks right, and Marketer takes it from there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Setup typically takes under 15 minutes. Once your accounts are connected and the first brief is approved, Marketer can have your first campaigns live within the hour — and it starts optimizing immediately after that.
</Tip>

## What happens while you sleep

The agent doesn't wait for business hours. Here's an example of a real overnight activity log from a protein bar brand on the platform:

| Time  | Action |
|-------|--------|
| 01:14 | Paused 2 underperforming ad sets draining budget with no conversions |
| 03:02 | Shifted $400 in daily budget from stalled campaigns to top performers |
| 06:45 | Cleaned 12 negative keywords on Google PMax to cut wasted spend |
| 08:00 | Morning brief sent to Slack with overnight summary and recommendations |
| 09:12 | You approved one recommended action from the brief |
| 09:14 | 4 new ads live across Meta and Google based on approved brief |

<Note>
  The agent begins running immediately after your first brief is approved — including overnight. You'll receive the morning brief in Slack each day summarising everything the agent did and flagging anything that needs your input.
</Note>
