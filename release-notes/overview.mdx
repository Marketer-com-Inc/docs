Release notes summarize changes that affect how you use Marketer. Each entry distinguishes a product change from a documentation-only update and calls out Beta availability when relevant.

## August 7, 2026

### New product documentation

**Documentation update**

We replaced the previous marketing-oriented site with a product manual organized around the way you adopt and operate Marketer.

- A new quickstart ends with a grounded, read-only first result.
- Essential guidelines explain context, source review, freshness, approvals, and autonomy.
- Product guides now cover Campaigns, Analytics, Creatives, Workflows, Knowledge and Skills, and Products.
- Integration guides distinguish supported providers from Beta providers.
- Workspace guidance covers roles, brand context, guardrails, audiences, notifications, billing, and security.
- Copyable use cases connect common requests to explicit review steps.

No product behavior changed as part of this documentation release.

## How release notes are maintained

An entry appears here when a customer-facing change affects setup, navigation, permissions, supported behavior, integration status, or a documented workflow. Small wording corrections may ship without a separate entry.

<Note>
  Availability can depend on your workspace, role, plan, connected accounts, or Beta access. The relevant guide describes those conditions when they apply.
</Note>

If the product does not match a guide, contact [Marketer Support](https://support.marketer.com) and include the page URL, workspace, and the behavior you observed. Do not include credentials or sensitive customer data.
