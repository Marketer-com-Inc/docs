## About Marketer

Marketer is on a mission to become the dominant platform for driving business growth through marketing and sales. We are focused on delivering real outcomes and results for our customers, helping them run campaigns, generate creatives, and understand their performance through AI-driven workflows. We are on a fast growth trajectory, building a global customer base with a large focus on e-commerce and service businesses. We are an AI-native company that works as a small, fast-moving team, keeping the distance between an idea and a shipped product short.

## About the Role

We are hiring a Product Designer to own product design end to end and raise the bar for craft across the experience. You will work in a dynamic environment alongside a fast-paced engineering team, bringing a pragmatic approach to how you design. This role suits a designer with strong product instincts and a natural inclination toward design engineering, comfortable getting into the technical details, prototyping with AI tools and Figma, and working with engineers so the shipped product reflects the original intent.

## Responsibilities

- Own the design of features end to end, from problem definition through prototyping to shipped polish.
- Steer the overall product experience toward simplicity and clarity.
- Build and maintain the design system so quality scales as we grow.
- Prototype quickly using AI tools and Figma, and in code where it sharpens the result.
- Engage with customers and internal stakeholders to gather feedback, and ground design decisions in their needs and pain points.
- Design data-dense, agentic interfaces such as streaming chat, approval flows, and analytics dashboards that stay clear under real-world data.

## Required Qualifications

- Experienced at UI design and crafting high-quality product experiences, with strong interaction, visual, and information design craft and a portfolio of shipped work in data-rich or tool-oriented products.
- Demonstrated product thinking with a pragmatic approach grounded in customer needs.
- A clear inclination toward design engineering: prototyping with AI tools and Figma.
- A self-starter who works autonomously while staying in sync with the team, comfortable with ambiguity and ownership.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Familiarity with performance marketing, advertising, or e-commerce.
- Proficiency in AI-driven front-end development, shipping production UI independently (React, TypeScript, modern CSS).
- Experience with data visualization, dashboards, or real-time/agentic interfaces.
- Experience establishing or owning a design system.

## Why Join

- As our founding designer, you will have real opportunity to shape the product experience from the ground up.
- Be part of a team dedicated to creating high-quality customer experiences.
- Take on interesting challenges, with real opportunities for growth in a future-oriented company.
- Work where design is a highly valued function, and something the whole team cares about.
