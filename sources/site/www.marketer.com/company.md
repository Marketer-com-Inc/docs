## Why Marketer.com exists

For most ecom brands, marketing runs on borrowed labor and disconnected tools. An agency does the buying, a freelancer ships the creative, three dashboards tell three different stories. The work never compounds because the data and the execution never sit in the same place. \[Marketer.com\](http://Marketer.com) replaces the model. Software runs the operation. The team runs the strategy.
