## What stops slowing the next drop down

### Drop-paced creative

Ember ships at drop pace, not photoshoot pace.

### Cross-channel push

Meta, Google, Snapchat from one upload, every market.

### Silhouette signals.

Performance reads which looks are converting, fed back into the next batch.

### Klaviyo connected.

Drop announcements firing from the same source of truth as the ads.

### Less handoff

Brief, ship, review, scale, all on one platform.

### AI Agent

Watches the launch for fatigue and budget leaks before the week ends.
