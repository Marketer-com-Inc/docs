## What stops blocking the next launch

### SKU coverage

Ember ships creative for every new flavor and pack size on cadence.

### Trial signals

Performance reads which offers convert cold buyers fastest, fed into the next round.

### Reorder flows

Klaviyo reads the same source of truth and fires replenishment automatically.

### Cross-channel push

Meta, Google, Snapchat live from one upload, every market, no double work.

### Launch cadence

Plan, brief, ship, push, all on one calendar timed to launch day.

### AI agent

Flags drop-off in the reorder cycle before LTV starts breaking.
