## One operating layer from inspiration to checkout.

Marketer runs awareness, retargeting, and lifecycle from one workspace. Ember keeps the lifestyle creative flowing so shoppers don't see the same ad three weeks in. Klaviyo nurtures from the same source of truth, with the email and the ad reading the same data. Attribution that holds up across a long path, not a 24-hour window.

## Inspiration that scales, on brand.

Ember ships static, video, and motion ads on subscription, curated by real designers who know lifestyle and decor. Briefed inside the platform. Lookbook, lifestyle scene, product hero, room context, all in rotation, all on brand. Volume sized for a long consideration cycle, not a 30-second launch.

## What stops getting lost in the consideration cycle

### Long-cycle creative

Ember keeps the lifestyle library fresh so retargeting doesn't burn out.

### Cycle signals

Performance reads what converts after three weeks, not just three hours.

### Nurture flows

Klaviyo warms shoppers from first save to first checkout on the same data.

### Cross-channel push

Meta, Google, Snapchat live from one upload, every market, no double work.

### Real attribution

The number reads the long path, not just the last click.

### AI agent

Flags drop-off in the consideration cycle before the audience cools.
