## What stops eating the retention curve

### Subscription signals

Performance reads who's renewing, who's churning, fed into the next batch.

### Creative variety

Testimonial, science, lifestyle, routine, all running in parallel rotation.

### Klaviyo retention

Trial, subscription, reactivation, all on the same source of truth.

### Trial mechanics

Sample, sub-discount, and bundle offers tested side-by-side to see what converts.

### Cross-channel push

Meta, Google, Snapchat live from one upload, every market, no double work.

### AI agent

Flags churn risk in the cohort before the renewal date.
