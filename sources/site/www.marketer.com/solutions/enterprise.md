Enterprise marketing is its own discipline. More work, more people, more moving parts. Marketer is the operating layer built for that complexity, so the team spends less time running the operation and more time doing the work they're hired for.

## One platform every team can actually use.

Connect your store. Marketer reads your data, sets up tracking, ships the first campaigns, and tells you what to do next. Meta, Google, and Snapchat in one place. Klaviyo on the same source of truth. Ember turns out static and video creative on demand, on-brand. The AI agent handles the things you wouldn't have time to learn.

## Depth without the learning curve.

New hires get productive in a day, not a quarter. Senior operators get the depth they expect. Briefs, performance reading, campaign setup, all in the same place. No cross-training to migrate someone from "knows the dashboard" to "ships campaigns." The platform behaves the same whether the account team is three people or thirty.
