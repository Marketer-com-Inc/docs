## One platform. The leverage of an in-house team.

Marketer consolidates the operation. Meta, Google, and Snapchat run from one layer. Klaviyo reads from the same data. Ember turns out the creative volume the team can't ship on its own. The AI agent handles the work that used to eat half the week, briefing, performance reading, flagging the ad sets that need attention before they bleed.

Ember ships static, video, and motion ads on subscription, curated by real designers, not just an AI. Briefed inside the platform, shipped at the cadence the algorithm wants. The team stops asking the agency for variants and starts deciding which winners to scale.
