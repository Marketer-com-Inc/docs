## Continuous research. Sharpened judgment.

Marketer runs creative research in the background, cultural, competitive, audience. Performance signals stream in alongside, so the work in production is always in view. The ads library updates automatically. The AI agent interrogates your brief against the data and surfaces the angles worth exploring. You stop building the inputs and start using them.

## Creative volume that doesn't insult the strategy.

Ember turns out static, video, and motion ads on subscription, curated by real designers, not just an AI. Briefed inside the platform. You hand off the direction, Ember ships, performance feeds back into the next round. The cycle finally closes.
