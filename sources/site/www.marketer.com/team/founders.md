## The brand needs you. The ad account doesn't.

You own the brand. You own the P&L. You probably don't want to own the ad accounts too. Marketer is the operating layer that runs the operation, with you signing off on what matters and skipping the rest.

## One platform you can actually trust to run.

Marketer takes the operation off your desk. Meta, Google, and Snapchat run from one layer. Klaviyo on the same source of truth. Ember ships on-brand creative without you proofing every JPEG. The AI agent watches performance, surfaces what needs your call, and quietly handles the rest.

![Marketing for Startups](https://www.marketer.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcdn.sanity.io%2Fimages%2Fh6m9n27w%2Fproduction%2F538cc7b0e3c303dad0ecb0d1e0134fb4d6fef3df-1460x1228.png%3Frect%3D239%2C0%2C982%2C1228%26w%3D640%26h%3D800%26q%3D75%26auto%3Dformat&w=3840&q=75)

## Creative that stays on brand. Without your inbox getting involved.

Ember produces static, video, and motion ads with brand governance built in, curated by real designers, not just an AI. Every output checked against your guidelines automatically. You stop being the last line of defense for "is this on brand?"
