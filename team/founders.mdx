You built something worth marketing. The problem is that the ad account has quietly become a second job — one you never hired for. Between monitoring performance, proofing creative, chasing attribution numbers, and fielding agency updates, the hours disappear before you've touched the actual brand. Marketer takes the entire operation off your desk. An AI agent watches your campaigns continuously, surfaces only the decisions that need your call, and handles the rest. Ember produces on-brand creative at volume without you proofing every JPEG — brand governance is built into every output. The brand gets your attention. The ad account doesn't.

## What You Still Control

Removing the operational burden doesn't mean handing over the wheel. You stay in command of everything that shapes the brand and the business.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set Your Brand Guidelines">
    Upload your brand kit once — logos, colours, fonts, tone of voice. Ember and the AI agent operate inside those guardrails from that point on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define Your Strategy and Budget">
    You decide where to play, how much to spend, and which channels to prioritise. Marketer executes inside the parameters you set.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve Material Actions">
    Significant budget moves or strategic pivots come to you for sign-off before anything changes. Everything else runs automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Performance on Your Terms">
    Revenue-tied reporting is always waiting when you want it — not packaged up by an agency on their schedule.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can run your entire ad operation from your phone in Slack. Marketer surfaces approvals, alerts, and performance summaries directly in your Slack workspace — so you stay in control without staying glued to a dashboard.
</Tip>
