The bottleneck in most media buying operations is never the strategy — it's the throughput. Creative requests go out, rounds come back, specs get rejected, naming conventions drift, and by the time a meaningful test is live, the algorithm has already moved on. Marketer is built around the test cycle: bulk uploads go direct to Meta and Google, format compliance is handled automatically, and winning performance data flows straight back into the next Ember brief. The loop closes. You stop losing weeks between insight and execution, and you stop doing the manual operations work that doesn't belong in a media buyer's day.

## The Media Buyer Workflow in Marketer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Bulk Upload Your Creatives">
    Upload static images, video, and motion assets in bulk directly from Marketer. Every file is pushed straight to Meta and Google — no manual ad creation, no platform switching, no copy-pasting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let Format and Spec Compliance Run Automatically">
    Marketer checks every asset against platform specs before it goes anywhere. Incorrect dimensions, wrong aspect ratios, and file-size violations are caught and corrected automatically. Rejected uploads stop happening.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply Naming Conventions at Upload">
    Naming conventions are enforced at the point of upload — not rebuilt from scratch every time someone new joins the account. Your reporting structure holds up on Friday, every Friday.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allocate Budget Across Audience Segments">
    Use the budget allocator to split spend across new-to-brand, engagement, and existing customer audiences exactly the way you want. Set your logic once — adjustments are applied consistently across every campaign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the Performance Loop">
    Winning creatives don't just sit in a dashboard. Their performance signals automatically brief the next Ember batch, so every production cycle starts with real data — not gut feel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Commission Fresh Variants from Ember">
    Ember delivers creative variants at the cadence the algorithm wants. Real designers work inside your brand parameters — static, video, UGC-style, motion — so the feed never runs dry.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The performance loop is the part that compounds. When a creative wins, Marketer captures what made it work — the angle, the format, the audience — and passes that signal directly into the next Ember brief. You don't have to write up the debrief. The cycle runs on its own.
</Tip>

## What You Stop Doing

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Chasing Creative Rounds" icon="envelope-open">
    Stop emailing the agency for round 14 of a static that should have shipped last week. Ember operates on a production schedule you set — not one you negotiate.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Copy-Pasting CSVs" icon="file-csv">
    Stop moving data between platforms by hand. Performance data, naming data, and budget data all live in one workspace and flow where they need to go automatically.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Re-uploading Rejected Assets" icon="rotate-left">
    Stop losing hours to platform rejections. Spec compliance runs before anything is submitted — assets either go live or get flagged with a clear reason before they hit the review queue.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Rebuilding Naming Conventions" icon="tag">
    Stop retraining every new hire on the naming logic. Conventions are baked into the workflow — they apply consistently regardless of who uploaded the file.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
