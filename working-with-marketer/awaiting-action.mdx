**Awaiting action** is the workspace inbox for decisions that still need a person. It brings pending approvals and proposals together so you can review them without finding the original conversation first.

Items appear here because Marketer needs an explicit decision, not because a change has already happened.

## What belongs here

Depending on your workspace configuration, the inbox can include:

- A proposed change to a connected account.
- An approval required by a workflow step.
- A proposal that needs acceptance or rejection.
- A decision that blocks a longer-running task.

The available controls depend on the proposal, connected provider, workspace role, and approval settings. Use the options shown on the item rather than assuming every proposal supports the same decisions.

## Review a proposal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm the workspace and source">
    Make sure the item belongs to the intended workspace and connected provider. Similar campaign or asset names are not enough to establish scope.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the exact proposed change">
    Identify the object, current state, proposed state, and size of the change. For campaign work, review the level involved and any effect on spend or delivery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the evidence">
    Open the supporting result or conversation. Confirm the date range, metrics, attribution source, currency, and freshness that justify the proposal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the guardrail">
    Compare the proposal with your workspace guardrails, autonomy settings, and the authority of the person making the decision.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide and verify">
    Use the available decision control. If the item proceeds, return to the owning product surface and confirm the resulting state after the provider has processed it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Approval checklist

For a change that can affect spend, targeting, delivery, or a public asset, verify all of the following:

- **Provider:** The intended connected account is selected.
- **Object:** The campaign, ad set, ad, workflow, or asset is unambiguous.
- **Effect:** You understand what will change and what will remain unchanged.
- **Magnitude:** The budget, status, audience, or delivery impact is acceptable.
- **Evidence:** The recommendation is supported by the intended measurement context.
- **Guardrail:** The change fits current workspace rules and business constraints.
- **Recovery plan:** You know what you will monitor and what would cause you to revisit the decision.

<Warning>
  Do not approve an item only because its title sounds familiar. Open the supporting detail and verify the exact object and effect, especially when multiple connected accounts use similar names.
</Warning>

## After a decision

The inbox is a decision surface, not the final source of truth for every product state.

| Decision relates to… | Verify in… |
| --- | --- |
| Campaign structure, status, or delivery | **Campaigns** and the connected provider state |
| Creative brief or generated asset | **Creatives** |
| Recurring report, alert, or action | **Workflows** and its run history |
| A longer analysis or research task | The task result and **Activity** |
| A reusable procedure | **Skills** |

If a proposal is rejected, return to its conversation or task when you want to revise the scope. A rejection should not be treated as an instruction to create a second, slightly different action automatically.

## Keep the inbox manageable

- Review pending decisions in a regular operating cadence.
- Resolve the highest-risk or time-sensitive items first.
- Reject stale proposals when their evidence window no longer supports the action.
- Avoid approving multiple overlapping changes before you can observe their combined effect.
- Use Notifications for receipts and alerts; keep **Awaiting action** focused on unresolved decisions.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A proposal has too little context">
    Open its linked conversation or task and request the source, measurement frame, current state, and expected effect. Do not approve until the decision is independently understandable.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The approval control is unavailable">
    Confirm your workspace role, the proposal state, and the health of the relevant connection. The item may also have been resolved by another authorized workspace member.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An approved change is not visible yet">
    Check the item state, task activity, and the owning surface. Provider processing and data refresh can take time. Avoid submitting a duplicate while the original action is still being resolved.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The proposal is based on stale data">
    Decline or leave it unresolved, refresh the underlying analysis after the source is current, and request a new proposal. Do not apply an old recommendation to a materially different account state.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
