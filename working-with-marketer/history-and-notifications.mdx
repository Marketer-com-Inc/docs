Marketer keeps different kinds of operational records separate. **Chat history** preserves workspace conversations. **Notifications** collects agent receipts, alerts, and approval-related updates. **Awaiting action** holds decisions that are still unresolved.

Choosing the right record helps you distinguish past context, current status, and work that still needs a person.

## Chat history

**Chat history** lists conversations in the current workspace. A thread shows its title, most recent activity, and, when available, who started it. Archived threads are marked in the list.

Use it to:

- Reopen a conversation and continue from its established context.
- Review the request, attachments, and decisions behind a result.
- Hand work between workspace members without rebuilding the full prompt.
- Start a new thread when an old context no longer fits the objective.

<Note>
  Threads belong to the workspace context. Before adding sensitive information, make sure it is appropriate for the people who can access that workspace.
</Note>

### Continue or start over

Continue an existing thread when the new work depends on its attachments, definitions, or prior decision. Start a new thread when:

- The objective changes materially.
- You need a different authority boundary.
- The prior thread contains conflicting date ranges or account scopes.
- You want a clean handoff or reusable record for a separate project.

When continuing a long thread, restate the current decision, measurement window, and constraints. Do not assume an older campaign state or product fact is still current.

## Notifications

**Notifications** is the workspace center for agent receipts, alerts, and approval-related updates. Use it to understand what Marketer reported and to follow a link back to the relevant work.

The feed can help you find:

- A receipt that a task or workflow produced an update.
- A performance or operational alert.
- A notice related to a proposal or approval.
- A link back to the record, conversation, or task that provides context.

You can mark notifications as read after you have reviewed them. Reading a notification does not approve an action, resolve a task, or confirm a provider-side change.

## Pick the right record

| Question | Open |
| --- | --- |
| “What did we ask and decide?” | **Chat history** |
| “What work is running or finished?” | **Activity** or the task detail |
| “What update or alert did Marketer send?” | **Notifications** |
| “What still needs a human decision?” | **Awaiting action** |
| “What is the current campaign, creative, or workflow state?” | The owning product surface |

## Review an operational update

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the source record">
    Follow the notification or history item to its conversation, task, or product record. A notification summary is not a replacement for the underlying detail.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm recency">
    Compare the update time with the data freshness and current provider state. Older notifications can remain useful history without describing the current situation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Separate receipt from decision">
    Determine whether the item reports completed work, recommends a next step, or requests approval. Only the last case belongs in **Awaiting action**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the loop">
    Review the result, make any required decision, and verify the resulting state in the product surface that owns it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Notification habits

- Keep high-signal alerts and approval updates visible.
- Review notification preferences when the feed is too noisy or misses important categories.
- Do not use unread state as a project-management status.
- Use the workflow run history for delivery and execution questions.
- Use chat titles and clear final summaries to make future search easier.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A recent thread is missing">
    Confirm the active workspace and check whether the thread is archived. If another workspace member created it, make sure you are looking in the same workspace context.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A notification does not explain what happened">
    Open the linked task, conversation, or product record. Look for the detailed result, source, and current state before drawing a conclusion from the summary text.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A notification is read but the decision is still pending">
    Read state and approval state are separate. Open **Awaiting action** and resolve the proposal through its available controls.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The feed is too noisy">
    Review workspace notification settings and recurring workflows. Keep the channels and categories that correspond to decisions your team will actually make.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
