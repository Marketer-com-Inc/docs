Marketer gives you one place to investigate performance, create marketing work, and review changes. You can start with the Marketing Agent or open a product surface when you want to inspect the underlying records directly.

The interface is organized around a simple operating loop: give Marketer the right context, let the work run, review the result, and decide whether any proposed action should proceed.

## The working loop

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start with a clear outcome">
    Tell Marketer what you want to decide or produce. Include the workspace scope, channel, date range, success measure, and any constraint that would change the answer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ground the request">
    Attach a relevant record in chat or open the product surface that owns it. Connected account data, workspace knowledge, and your explicit context should agree before you rely on the result.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the work">
    A quick analysis may finish in the conversation. Longer work can appear as a task with its own progress. Treat the task status, not elapsed time, as the indication of whether the work is finished.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the result">
    Check the source, date window, attribution view, currency, and assumptions behind any recommendation. For generated creative, also review brand accuracy, product facts, and policy-sensitive claims.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make the decision">
    Read-only work can end with an answer or artifact. A change to a connected account may require a separate approval. Review the exact provider, object, scope, and effect before you approve it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose the right surface

| You want to… | Start in… | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ask a cross-channel question | **Home** and the Marketing Agent | The conversation can combine context and coordinate the next step. |
| Inspect or manage advertising objects | **Campaigns** | You can work from the actual campaign, ad set, or ad records available for a connected provider. |
| Analyze results | **Analytics** | Performance, Creative performance, Reports, and Live view keep the measurement context visible. |
| Plan or make creative | **Creatives** | Briefs, Studio, and Assets cover planning, generation, and the asset library. |
| Configure recurring work | **Workflows** | You can review supported options, required connections, configuration, and run history. |
| Review brand context or procedures | **Knowledge** and **Skills** | Knowledge holds source material and learned context; Skills hold reusable ways of working. |
| Work from a Shopify catalog | **Products** | The catalog provides product identity, merchandising context, and entry points into campaigns or creative. |
| Decide on a proposed action | **Awaiting action** | Pending decisions are collected in one workspace inbox. |

<Note>
  The controls and actions you see depend on your connected providers, workspace role, plan, and approval settings. If a control is absent, confirm those conditions before assuming the capability is unavailable everywhere.
</Note>

## Common outcomes

You can use the same loop for many kinds of work:

- Explain why a performance metric changed and identify the strongest evidence.
- Compare campaigns or creatives over a defined period.
- Turn a product and audience goal into a creative brief.
- Produce creative options, then review and organize the resulting assets.
- Prepare a campaign draft and inspect it before launch.
- Configure a supported report, alert, or recurring workflow.
- Save stable brand facts in Knowledge and recurring procedures in Skills.

## Review before you act

Use a higher review bar when a result could change spend, delivery, targeting, or a public-facing asset.

1. Confirm the workspace and connected account.
2. Confirm the date window and measurement source.
3. Separate observed facts from interpretation.
4. Check the proposed object and the size of the change.
5. Verify who must approve and what happens after approval.
6. Return to the relevant surface after the action to confirm the resulting state.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The answer is too general">
    Add a specific record, channel, date range, and decision to the request. In chat, use the context picker to attach a campaign, brief, creative, or product instead of relying on a name alone.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The numbers do not match another view">
    Compare the provider, account, date boundary, reporting timezone, currency, attribution source, and freshness state. Two correct views can differ when those inputs differ.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Work appears to have stopped">
    Open the task or activity detail and look for a request for input, a failed prerequisite, or an incomplete connection. If a decision is required, check **Awaiting action**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An action is not available">
    Confirm that the relevant provider is connected and healthy, your role allows the action, and the workspace approval settings permit it. Available actions are provider-specific.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
