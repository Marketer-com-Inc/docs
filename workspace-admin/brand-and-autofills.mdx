title: "Brand profile and autofills"
description: "Give Marketer approved brand context and reusable launch defaults."
---

Branding gives Marketer the context it should use. Autofills reduce repeated setup when your team creates campaigns and ads.

## Build the brand profile

Open **Settings** → **Branding & Brand Profile**.

The page includes:

- **Workspace name** and reporting currency;
- brand-kit sources from a website or uploaded guidelines;
- proposed brand facts for review;
- an approved brand profile used by the agent; and
- a competitor watchlist supplied by your team.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add reliable sources">
    Point Marketer at your current website or upload approved brand guidelines. Prefer material your team already treats as authoritative.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review proposed facts">
    Approve, adjust, or deny each fact. Do not approve a statement only because it sounds plausible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check workspace identity">
    Confirm the workspace name and reporting currency. Currency affects how blended reporting is presented.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Maintain competitors">
    Add only competitors the team intentionally monitors. Remove stale entries when positioning changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Approved facts are the working brand context Marketer uses for writing and targeting. Keep source material current and revisit facts after a rebrand, market change, or major product launch.

## Configure autofills

Open **Settings** → **Autofills**. Defaults are organized into four areas:

| Area | What it pre-fills |
| --- | --- |
| **Advantage+ enhancements** | Saved creative enhancement presets for new Meta ads |
| **Naming presets** | Consistent campaign, ad set, and ad naming patterns |
| **Copy** | Reusable ad-copy defaults |
| **Website & UTM** | Destination and tracking defaults |

New campaign flows inherit the selected defaults. A teammate can still review and override them for an individual launch.

An optional Slack drift alert can notify the team when live Advantage+ settings move away from the saved expectation. Slack must be connected and able to list the chosen channel.

## Review checklist

- Brand facts are approved by someone who owns the messaging.
- Reporting currency matches the workspace's intended reporting unit.
- Naming presets are legible outside Marketer.
- Default copy does not contain a temporary offer or expired claim.
- Destination and UTM values are valid for the current site.
- Creative defaults are reviewed in the launch flow before publishing.
