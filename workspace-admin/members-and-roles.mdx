People belong to an account. Their access can cover every workspace or only selected workspaces.

## Role model

| Role | Typical access |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Full control, including workspace settings and ownership-level billing or seat decisions |
| **Admin** | Broad editing access and team management within the assigned account or workspace scope |
| **Member** | Create and edit workspace content |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access |

The same label can be account-wide or attached to a specific workspace grant. Always review both the role and its reach.

<Note>
  Billing visibility and controls vary by role, workspace billing model, and whether the workspace is covered by an account plan. Viewers do not see **Plans & Billing** in workspace settings.
</Note>

## Invite a teammate

Only account Owners and Admins can invite teammates or change roles.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Members">
    Go to **Settings** → **Members**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add one or more emails">
    Enter the teammates you intend to invite. Marketer prevents duplicate, pending, self, and existing-member invitations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose access mode">
    Grant one role across **All workspaces**, or choose **Selected workspaces** and set a role for each workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review MCP access separately">
    If the teammate needs MCP, review seat availability and billing ownership. Workspace access and an MCP seat are related but separate grants.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send and monitor">
    Send the invite. It remains under **Pending invites** until accepted.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage existing members

For account-wide access, an Owner or Admin can change the member's all-workspace role. For selected access, expand the member and choose **No access**, Owner, Admin, Member, or Viewer for each workspace.

Removing a teammate from the account immediately removes their workspace access. Review active work, ownership, and MCP sessions first.

## MCP seats

MCP seats are assigned per member and workspace unless the effective plan provides unlimited seats. Account owners control seat changes that can affect billing. Giving someone workspace access does not automatically authorize their external agent connection.

## Access review checklist

- Every person uses an individual account.
- Viewers are used for read-only review.
- Selected-workspace access is preferred when someone does not need every workspace.
- Former teammates are removed promptly.
- Pending invites are canceled when no longer valid.
- MCP seats and connected OAuth sessions match current responsibilities.
