Marketer has three administrative scopes. Confirm the scope before changing access, policy, routing, or billing.

## Scope map

| Scope | Follows | Customer-visible areas |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Personal** | Your Marketer identity across workspaces | **Profile**, **Authentication**, **MCP connect**, **Refer & earn** |
| **Workspace** | The active workspace in the switcher | Branding, Autofills, Members' workspace grants, Guardrails, Autonomy, audiences, conversion metrics, Notifications, Plans & Billing, integrations |
| **Account** | A qualifying multi-workspace account | **Overview**, **Analytics**, **Billing**, **Users**, **MCP** |

<Warning>
  A page can look identical across workspaces while controlling different provider accounts, policies, audiences, or billing. Confirm the active workspace before every material change.
</Warning>

## Settings map

| Area | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile** | Full name, profile photo, and read-only account email |
| **Authentication** | Sign-in identities, password reset, and **Sign out everywhere** |
| **MCP connect** | Personal connected agents, scopes, and workspace grants |
| **Branding & Brand Profile** | Workspace identity, approved brand facts, sources, and competitors |
| **Autofills** | Advantage+ defaults, naming presets, ad copy, destination, and UTM defaults |
| **Members** | Account members, invitations, workspace reach, roles, and MCP seats |
| **Guardrails** | Performance targets, currency-bound spend caps, scaling limits, and protected campaigns |
| **Autonomy** | Default creation mode and the launch review stop |
| **Audience segments** | Reusable Meta segment definitions and reporting roles |
| **Custom audiences** | Mirrored Meta Custom Audiences and lookalikes |
| **Conversion metrics** | Selected conversion actions shown in Analytics |
| **Notifications** | Slack and email delivery, severity floor, and event groups |
| **Plans & Billing** | Workspace plan, tokens, usage, payment method, invoices, and workspace deletion |

## Role basics

| Role | Workspace posture |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Full workspace control |
| **Admin** | Broad editing and workspace people management |
| **Member** | Creates and edits workspace content and settings |
| **Viewer** | Read-only; mutation controls are disabled and **Plans & Billing** is hidden |

Account-wide and selected-workspace grants can use the same labels. Always review both the role and its reach in [Members and roles](/workspace-admin/members-and-roles).

## Move between scopes

- Use the top-left workspace switcher for active-workspace context. See [Workspace switching](/workspace-admin/workspace-switching).
- Eligible account Owners and Admins with at least two active workspaces see **Account overview**. See [Account overview](/workspace-admin/account-overview).
- **MCP connect** in personal settings is account-spanning, even though each connection reaches only the workspaces you grant.
- Account **Users** reuses the same account-grain team model as workspace **Members**; it is not a second membership system.

## Recommended setup order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm identity and scope">
    Review Profile, Authentication, active workspace, and effective role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set membership">
    Invite people with the narrowest all-workspace or selected-workspace reach they need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ground the workspace">
    Build the brand kit, review proposed facts, and configure campaign defaults.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set policy">
    Define guardrails and keep **Review everything** while testing real outputs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect and route">
    Connect providers, verify coverage, and configure notification delivery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review cost and external access">
    Confirm workspace-versus-account billing and review MCP seats and sessions.
  </Step>
</Steps>
