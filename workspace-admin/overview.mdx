Settings in Marketer are divided by who or what they affect. Personal settings follow your account. Workspace settings shape the active workspace. Eligible multi-workspace owners and admins also see an account-level overview.

## Settings map

| Area | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile** | Your name, profile photo, and account email |
| **Authentication** | Sign-in identities, password reset, and global sign-out |
| **MCP connect** | Connected agents, access scopes, and workspace grants |
| **Branding & Brand Profile** | Workspace identity, brand facts, sources, and competitors |
| **Autofills** | Defaults for campaign creation |
| **Members** | Account membership, roles, workspace access, invites, and MCP seats |
| **Guardrails** | Performance targets, spend limits, approval thresholds, and protected campaigns |
| **Autonomy** | Default brief mode and whether accepted launches require a review stop |
| **Audience segments** | Meta reporting segment definitions by ad account |
| **Custom audiences** | Meta Custom Audience and lookalike management |
| **Conversion metrics** | Workspace-selected conversion actions shown in Analytics when available |
| **Notifications** | In-app, Slack, and email delivery preferences |
| **Plans & Billing** | Agent activity, plan, usage, payment method, and invoices when applicable |

## Personal, workspace, and account scope

**Personal settings** belong to you. Profile, Authentication, and MCP connect are not tied to one selected workspace.

**Workspace settings** apply to the active workspace. Confirm the workspace before changing brand context, defaults, members' workspace access, guardrails, autonomy, audiences, conversion metrics, notifications, or billing.

**Account settings** appear only for eligible owners and admins of a multi-workspace account. That area provides **Overview**, **Analytics**, **Billing**, **Users**, and **MCP** across the account's workspaces.

## Role basics

- **Owner** has full control, including workspace settings.
- **Admin** can edit and manage people for the workspace or account scope they have.
- **Member** can create and edit workspace content.
- **Viewer** is read-only.

Exact visibility and billing controls also depend on whether access is account-wide or limited to selected workspaces. See [Members and roles](/workspace-admin/members-and-roles).

## Safe setup order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set identity and access">
    Review your profile, authentication, team membership, and workspace roles.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ground the workspace">
    Add brand sources, review proposed brand facts, and configure reusable defaults.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set policy before automation">
    Define guardrails and keep **Review everything** until the team has reviewed real outputs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure delivery">
    Choose notification channels, recipients, and severity thresholds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review usage and ownership">
    Confirm who owns billing, which workspaces are account-covered, and who can manage MCP seats.
  </Step>
</Steps>
