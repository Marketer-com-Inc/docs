Profile and Authentication are personal settings. They follow your account across every workspace you can access.

## Update your profile

Open **Settings** → **Profile** to manage:

- **Profile photo** — upload, replace, or remove the image teammates see.
- **Full name** — the name shown in shared workspaces.
- **Email** — the current sign-in email, shown as read-only.

Profile images accept the formats and size shown beside the upload control. A saved name or photo becomes visible to teammates after the page refreshes.

<Note>
  The Profile page does not currently change your account email. Follow the support or reauthentication process shown in the product if the email must change.
</Note>

## Review authentication

Open **Settings** → **Authentication** to review:

- the email on the account;
- the most recent sign-in time;
- linked identity providers and when they were last used;
- the password-reset action; and
- **Sign out everywhere**.

### Reset your password

Select **Send reset link**. Marketer sends the reset flow to the email on the account. Complete it from a device and browser you trust.

### Sign out every session

Use **Sign out everywhere** when a device is lost, a session is no longer trusted, or you want to force a fresh sign-in across browsers and phones.

<Warning>
  Global sign-out revokes every active session for your account, including the session you are using. Save work in progress first.
</Warning>

## Sign-in troubleshooting

1. Confirm that you are using the same email or linked identity shown on the Authentication page.
2. Use the password-reset flow rather than repeatedly guessing a password.
3. Complete verification in the same browser where you began sign-in when possible.
4. If you can sign in but cannot reach a workspace, ask an account Owner or Admin to review your membership and workspace grant.
5. If access changed unexpectedly, sign out everywhere and start a fresh session before escalating.
